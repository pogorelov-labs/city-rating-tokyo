# Bangkok safety sources — BMA risk-area registry, police data, road safety, homeless services (CRTKY-150)

Date: 2026-10-13. Researcher: automated open-data sweep for CRTKY-150.
Status: research only — nothing here is adopted; any integration is an owner decision.

## Access notes (what worked, what didn't)

- `data.bangkok.go.th` (CKAN 2.9.9, "Open Government Data of Bangkok", 1,368 datasets, 118
  organisations) **rejects direct TLS from this machine**: `curl`/`urllib` fail with
  `SSL_ERROR_SYSCALL`, WebFetch times out (tried plain and with a browser User-Agent).
  Server-side readers work: the `web_reader` MCP tool and the Exa fetcher return both the
  HTML pages and the CKAN JSON API (`/api/3/action/package_search`,
  `/api/3/action/datastore_search`). Any future ingest script must assume the portal may
  block bots — it did for us.
- `opendata.thaipoliceonline.go.th` (CKAN 2.9.9, "Police Data Catalog") **is directly
  reachable** — plain `curl` works.
- Overpass API: `overpass-api.de` flaky from here (406 then TLS errors); mirrors
  `overpass.kumi.systems` and `overpass.private.coffee` worked.
- Files that the CKAN catalog serves as raw XLSX/PDF downloads could **not** be retrieved
  (Exa refuses the content type, `r.jina.ai` times out at 15 s, direct download is
  TLS-blocked). Field-level content of those resources is therefore **unverified** —
  stated as such below.

## 1. BMA "4 ปลอดภัยดี" (d4) group — the safety group

https://data.bangkok.go.th/group/d4 — **70 datasets** as of 2026-10-13 (the portal front
page still advertises 59; group page is current). Besides the registries below it also
carries: public street-lighting repair/installation budgets
(ข้อมูลงบประมาณการติดตั้งและซ่อมแซมไฟฟ้าส่องสว่างสาธารณะ), sidewalk repair distances
(P026), fire-station locations (per district), communities with drug spread, CCTV traffic
camera positions. None carry a per-district crime *rate*.

### 1a. บัญชีพื้นที่เสี่ยง ปี 2568 — Risk Area Registry 2025 — PRIMARY SOURCE, verified in depth

- Dataset: slug `ced0066`, id `aa1d9beb-3b71-4f5c-b7c3-d3af6b5399ca`.
- Description (translated): risk points in Bangkok, broken down by district office
  (สำนักงานเขต), surveyed in BE 2568 (= 2025) under the "survey and assessment of areas
  at risk of crime" project (โครงการสำรวจและประเมินพื้นที่เสี่ยงต่อการเกิดอาชญากรรม).
- Organisation: สำนักเทศกิจ (Civic Affairs Department). Group: d4. Type: record data.
  Updated 2026-09-24; update frequency: yearly; language: Thai.
- **License: "License not specified"** (`isopen: false`). This is the rule, not the
  exception, on this portal — most BMA datasets carry no explicit licence.
- Resource: `14-69.csv`, 52,298 bytes, `datastore_active: true` → read the whole table
  through `datastore_search` (method: CKAN API via Exa, 2026-10-13).

**What is actually inside (queried live, 216 rows total):**

| Field | Thai label | Content seen |
|---|---|---|
| `District` | สำนักงานเขต | district name in Thai, e.g. คลองเตย |
| `Place name` | ชื่อสถานที่ | free text, e.g. "สวนเฉลิมพระเกรียติ 72 พรรษา ซอยสุขุมวิท 50" |
| `Type of place` | ประเภทสถานที่ | สาธารณะ (public) / เอกชน (private) / รัฐวิสาหกิจ … |
| `Characteristics_place` | ลักษณะสถานที่ | park, pedestrian/motorbike footbridge, sidewalk, road, alley (ซอย), empty land, school … |
| `Type of risk` | ลักษณะความเสี่ยง/อันตราย | numbered category, see below |

- **No coordinates.** No lat/lng or geojson field; rows are *named places* only
  (many include road/soi names, so geocoding is possible but unverified in quality).
- **Coverage: exactly 50 distinct districts** (verified via `distinct=true`, total=50) —
  every khet has ≥ 1 row. 216 rows ÷ 50 districts ≈ 4.3 points per district.
- **Exactly 7 risk categories** (distinct values, total=7):

  1. จุดมืด จุดเปลี่ยว — dark, deserted spots
  2. จุดรกร้าง/พื้นที่ลักลอบทิ้งขยะ — abandoned spots / illegal dumping
  3. โครงสร้างวัสดุชำรุดรอซ่อมแซม — dilapidated structures awaiting repair
  4. แหล่งมั่วสุม — loitering/gathering spots
  5. จุดล่อแหลมต่อการเกิดอาชญากรรม — crime-prone points
  6. ประชากรหนาแน่น/พลุกพล่าน — dense/crowded areas
  7. จุดที่กำลังดำเนินการแก้ไขต่อการเกิดอุบัติเหตุ — accident-prone points being fixed

- Sample rows read (first 8 + CSV page through row ~59): Khlong Toei rows are parks,
  footbridges (Benjakitti frontage) and a shrine on Ratchadaphisek, all category 4;
  Khlong San has a U-turn point under category 7; Bang Khun Thian/Kannayao rows are dark
  alleys and village entrances, category 1. Multi-value cells exist in
  `Characteristics_place` (quoted CSV with commas inside).

**Reading of the data:** it is an *administrative remediation register*, not a crime
incident table. Counts are whatever each district office chose to register in its 2025
sweep; one of seven categories (6) isn't a risk at all for a resident and two (2, 3) are
blight, one (7) is road safety. Use it as **facts and texture, not as a score input**.

## 2. Law/courts/crime group — ความชุกของอาชญากรรม (crime prevalence)

- Dataset id `9c7b779f-8ace-4ef8-a998-899c6e771d78` (groups: d4 + กฎหมาย ศาล
  และอาชญากรรม + BMA city development indicators). License: not specified.
- Single resource: **PDF** "สถิติฐานความผิดคดีอาญา (คดี 4 กลุ่ม)" (criminal-case
  statistics, 4 case groups), 36,881 bytes, data source: **สำนักงานตำรวจแห่งชาติ (Royal
  Thai Police)**, data year 2020, published 2021-09, geo coverage field literally
  "ไม่มี" (none).
- Proxy definition per the dataset notes: count of non-serious/serious crime cases in the
  BMA area, referenced to Safe Cities Index 2019 / EIU Liveability Index 2019.
- **Verdict: city-level only. There is no district-level official crime statistic in any
  BMA group** — this directly confirms the honesty note already in
  `research/bangkok/00-overview.md` ("the police publish no district crime statistics").

## 3. opendata.thaipoliceonline.go.th — Royal Thai Police open data

Queried live 2026-10-13 (`package_search?rows=100`, `datastore_search`):

- **15 datasets, all from one bureau:** บช.สอท. — the Cyber Crime Investigation Bureau.
  Everything is online-scam/cyber-crime statistics: cases by type, by age group, by sex,
  by occupation, by damage value, by agency; cumulative online-case reports (some as PNG
  images, not data).
- The only "station-level" dataset — no11, สถิติจำนวนคดีที่ทางสถานีตำรวจได้รับแบ่งตามกลุ่มอายุ
  (cases received by police station, by age group), 2566 annual resource queried — has
  **99 rows keyed by หน่วยงาน = agency abbreviation** (ตร. = national police HQ,
  บช.ก. = Metropolitan Police Bureau, …), fields: agency, age group, cases, cases
  (male/female/unspecified). *Not* a list of the ~90 Bangkok สน. stations; no geography.
- **Verdict: reject** as a safety source. No street crime, no station or district
  geography. License mostly "License not specified" (one test dataset CC BY-SA).

## 4. Road safety — district-level data?

16 datasets match อุบัติเหตุ (accidents) on the BMA portal. Verified metadata:

- **จุดเสี่ยงภัยด้านการจราจรในพื้นที่กรุงเทพมหานคร ปี 2568** — slug
  `bkk_accident_risk`, id `c73f160b…`, Traffic and Transport Dept (TTD). geo coverage
  field = **พิกัด (coordinates)** — i.e. geo-referenced risk points, yearly. Resource:
  XLSX 24,642 B, datastore inactive; **the XLSX itself could not be fetched through any
  available channel (see access notes), so its columns are unverified.**
- **ข้อมูลการวิเคราะห์ Solution จุดเสี่ยงอุบัติเหตุบนถนน** (TTD): "Risk Map, 100 risk
  clusters" from ThaiRsc/iTIC, explicitly "มีพิกัดแผนที่ … จำนวนผู้เสียชีวิต และผู้บาดเจ็บ"
  — coordinates + deaths + injuries. 3,066 views. Contents not opened (file download).
- **สถิติการเกิดอุบัติเหตุที่เกิดขึ้นบนท้องถนน** (TTD, updated 2026-08): road-death
  counts in Bangkok — city-level.
- **ผลการดำเนินการแก้ไขจุดเสี่ยงอุบัติเหตุบนถนน ปี 2567** (TTD): remediation results
  **แยกรายเขต (broken down by district)** — a district-level *count of fixed spots*, not
  an accident rate.
- **จุดติดตั้งกล้อง CCTV เพื่อการจราจร** (City Planning, updated 2026-09): traffic CCTV
  camera positions (intersections, bus stops, risk spots).

**Answer:** there is no district accident-rate table, but there are geo-coded risk-point
lists (2568 register, 100-cluster risk map) and a per-district remediation count.
A district "road-risk point count" is derivable if the owner accepts a manual XLSX fetch.
Note the safety registry itself already carries road risk as category 7.

## 5. Police presence (OSM amenity=police) — feasible minor subsignal

Ran Overpass count queries on 2026-10-13:

```
[out:json];area["name:en"="Bangkok"]["admin_level"="4"]->.a;
nwr["amenity"="police"](area.a); out count;
```
→ **104 objects** (47 nodes + 56 ways + 1 relation; kumi mirror, OSM base 2026-06-01, ODbL).

Same pattern as Tokyo koban (`research/02-safety.md` §4a): count within walking radius as
a small positive subsignal. Bangkok's สน. stations are tagged; police boxes (กช.) are
sparse, so absolute numbers stay low — keep the weight minor, same doctrine as Tokyo.

## 6. Homeless services — framing only, publication is a separate owner decision

**Open data:** the portal has **no city-wide shelter/service-point dataset**. The only
homeless dataset is *จำนวนครั้งในการออกตรวจคนไร้บ้าน ปี 2568* — counts of homeless
patrol/inspection rounds by the Civic Affairs Dept and district offices (updated
2026-09-24, d4 group) — enforcement activity, unsuitable as a "services" signal.
Searches for ศูนย์คุ้มครองคนไร้บ้าน / shelter data return nothing.

**Named facilities (from BMA sources, EN framing material):**
- **บ้านอิ่มใจ (Baan Im Jai)** — BMA's emergency shelter + one-stop welfare centre on the
  old Metropolitan Waterworks site (44 rai), Ban Bat, Pom Prap Sattru Phai; ~200 places;
  free lodging ≤ 7 nights/month, opened Feb 2026 (policy.bangkok.go.th P049;
  bangkokbiznews 2025-05-14 and 2026-03-19; thestandard.co 2026-02-27).
- **Drop-in service points**: under Saphan Somdet Phra Pinklao (Phra Nakhon side),
  10:00–16:00 daily; and Trok Sake, Phra Nakhon, 17:00–22:00 daily (RYT9 press release;
  bangkokbiznews).
- **ศูนย์พักกาย** — temporary shelters planned/rolled out in 6 zone groups across the
  city (policy P049, สำนักพัฒนาสังคม, self-reported 99 %).
- NGO partners named by BMA: Issarachon Foundation (มูลนิธิอิสรชน), Mirror Foundation
  (กระจกเงา), Housing Development Foundation, Bangkok Community Health Foundation,
  บ้านมิตรไมตรี (Baan Mit Maitree; branches Huai Khwang, Sai Mai, On Nut, Thonburi);
  national DSD (พม.) ศูนย์คุ้มครองคนไร้ที่พึ่ง network.
- Scale context: BMA/DSD headcount of 23 May 2023 found 1,271 homeless people in
  Bangkok, 588 of them in Phra Nakhon district alone (RYT9, citing BMA Social
  Development Dept).

**OSM `amenity=social_facility` in Bangkok: 16 objects total** (4 nodes + 12 ways;
private.coffee mirror, base 2026-05-31) — far too sparse to model "proximity to
services" from OSM alone. A homeless-tagged subset query
(`social_facility:for~homeless|street`) timed out on every mirror — count not obtained,
ceiling 16. A hand-curated list of the ~10 named facilities above is the only workable
input. Per the ticket: proximity framing in descriptions only, **no scores**, and
publication is the owner's separate decision.

## 7. Can the registry blend into editorial-safety (e-Stat doctrine, `moderate`)?

Doctrine reference: `research/decisions/2026-09-30-epic80-checkpoint.md` (D3) — official
data enters as a *labelled* input with `moderate` confidence and a cap, never silently.
Current Bangkok safety is `editorial` ×50
(`scripts/bangkok/build.py:1161`, `data/bangkok/editorial.json`, values 1–10 per district).

My reading, grounded in what the register actually contains (§1a):

- **As a score input: not recommended.** 216 administratively-designated points
  (≈ 4.3/district) whose categories mix crime, blight, crowding and road works, with no
  coordinates and no licence — district rankings would swing on what each district office
  happened to register in 2025, not on lived safety. This is qualitatively different from
  the e-Stat rent case (a real municipal statistical series).
- **As facts/texture: yes.** Counts and named places are quotable on district pages
  ("district office lists 12 risk spots — mostly dark footbridges and parks"), and the
  register is a good validation/cross-check against the editorial assessment (e.g. does
  an editorially low-safety district really carry more registered spots?). Street-lighting
  budget and sidewalk-repair data in the same group serve the same purpose.
- If the owner still wants a blend: keep editorial dominant, add the registry-derived
  subsignal (e.g. crime-category share of points) at low weight, label `moderate` with a
  cap, and disclose the administrative provenance on the page. That is an implementation
  decision for the owner, not this research.
