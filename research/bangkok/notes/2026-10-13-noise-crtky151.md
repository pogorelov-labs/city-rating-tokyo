# Bangkok noise with a day/night dimension — PCD Noise Monitor (CRTKY-151)

Researched: **2026-10-13**, by the open-data research agent. Companion to
**CRTKY-146** (Air4Thai) — the ask said to judge the noise network with the
same question form; see §9 for the shared finding. All fetches below were run
by me in this session; commands and outputs are quoted. Nothing here is an
implementation decision — the owner adopts sources.

**TL;DR:** The PCD noise network is real, live and typed (roadside
`ริมถนน` vs community `ชุมชน`), but **Bangkok has only 7 stations, of which 5
reported on the fetch date**, and **no public hourly archive exists** — the
station page gives a 7-day daily Leq table plus a *latest-hour* snapshot
(Leq/L90/Lmin/Lmax). A true day(07–22)/night(22–06) split **cannot be built
from public data** (checked the page, query params, and the one JSON API I
found). What IS public: daily 24 h Leq for every station (7 days live; a full
2016–2025 daily archive embedded in dead station st11's HTML; annual
station-level tables 2019–2024 as PDFs on the site; annual CSVs 2016–2025 on
gdcatalog, license **Open Data Common**). Verdict for the ticket:
**partial** — adopt as *measured facts at stations* (annotations + validation
anchors), build the day/night quietness split as a **modeled** proxy
(nightlife density + major roads, already in the pipeline's OSM/Overture
stock), reject interpolation.

## 1. Live network — noisemonitor.net / noise4thai.com (same portal, two hosts)

- Portal: `https://noisemonitor.net/web/index.php` ("Noise Monitor in
  Thailand", Pollution Control Department / กรมควบคุมมลพิษ, footer
  "Copyright © 2016 - 2022 … กองจัดการคุณภาพอากาศและเสียง"). Station pages:
  `https://noise4thai.com/web/station.php?stationID=stXX` — the ask's two URLs
  are one and the same application (same tables, same station IDs).
- **TLS:** both hosts serve an **expired certificate for `air4thai.net`**
  (verified: `openssl s_client … | openssl x509 -noout -dates -subject` →
  `notAfter=Dec 7 23:59:59 2022 GMT, subject=CN=air4thai.net`). WebFetch
  refuses both (`certificate has expired (CERT_HAS_EXPIRED)`); fetching
  requires `curl -k`. This is the **same infrastructure as Air4Thai
  (CRTKY-146)** — see §9.
- Contact printed on every page: Noise & Vibration Section, PCD,
  `noise@pcd.go.th`, tel 0-2298-2325-29. Disclaimer on every page:
  "ข้อมูลระดับเสียงรายวันเป็นข้อมูลที่ผ่านการตรวจสอบเบื้องต้นเท่านั้น" — *daily
  values are preliminary (not yet QA-reviewed)*. No license text is shown on
  the portal itself; the open-data release of the same data is licensed (§3).

### 1.2 What a station page actually exposes (checked st52/st09/st53/st11 …)

From `station.php?stationID=…` HTML (fetched with
`curl -sk "https://noise4thai.com/web/station.php?stationID=st52"`):

1. **Latest hour**: `Leq เฉลี่ย 1 ชั่วโมง` + `L90`, `Lmin`, `Lmax` (one
   snapshot, e.g. st52 @06:00 → 67.7/58.3/49.4/87.1 dBA).
2. **7-day daily table**: one row per day, timestamped 06:00, columns
   `เวลาอัพเดท | 24 ชม. Leq | ชั่วโมงล่าสุด: Leq, L90, Lmin, Lmax` — e.g.
   st52: 7 Oct 71.6 | 68.1/59.9/49.3/85.1 … 13 Oct 70.6 | …
3. **Highcharts daily series** `var LeqDayInMonth = [[ts, Leq24h, hours], …]`
   — 7 points for every station I checked except st11 (§2.2).
4. **No hourly history.** Probes that failed to reveal one:
   `station.php?stationID=st52&year=2025&type=hour` → identical 7-day page;
   `data.php`, `json/noise_stXX.json` → 404; no AJAX endpoint in the HTML
   (grep for `url|get|post|load … .php|.json` → empty). **Consequence: the
   day(07–22)/night(22–06) split the ticket asks about cannot be computed
   from the portal.** The only night-ish public signal is the per-day
   "latest hour" column — for most stations that hour is the 05:00–06:00
   upload hour (all 6 daily rows I checked for st52/st09 are stamped 06:00;
   st10's stale row is stamped 10:00), i.e. a **single nightly-hour sample,
   not a night average**.
5. The ask's Thai station types are confirmed exactly as the portal prints
   them: `ริมถนน` (roadside) and `ชุมชน` (community/general area).

### 2.2 The st11 quirk — a full daily archive in one page

`station.php?stationID=st11` returned **541,887 bytes** (vs ~21 KB for every
other station). Its `LeqDayInMonth` array holds **3,652 daily points,
2 Oct 2014 → (non-zero) 2 Sep 2016 → 2 May 2025**: 3,007 days with data,
including all 366 days of 2024 (values 62.8–75.3 dBA sampled). So the *daily
24 h history exists server-side* and leaks through for this discontinued
station — but not for any other ID I fetched (ns01/st22/st35/st47 all carry
exactly 7 points). Worth asking PCD (`noise@pcd.go.th`) for a bulk export;
it may simply be unpublished.

### 2.4 Woonkathi pilot API — the only JSON endpoint I found

`noisemonitor.net/woonkathi` is a Vue SPA ("redcross_map"); its JS

`http://noise4thai.com/woonkathi/kachadAPI.php`. I called it (HTTP 200,

## 4. Day/night — what the data can and cannot give

**Cannot:** a Leq day(07–22)/night(22–06) split. No public endpoint carries
hourly history (§1.2, §2.4); the annual CSVs/PDFs are 24 h means; the monthly
JPGs are 24 h means. Thai noise standards *do* define day/night periods, and
PCD's manual survey reports contain Ld/Ln — but those surveys are not in
this open data.

**Can (measured, observed 2026-10-13):** the daily table's "latest hour"
column is a nightly-hour (≈05–06) sample next to the day's 24 h mean:

| Station (type) | 24 h Leq (7–10 Oct) | 06:00-hour Leq | gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| st52 roadside | 71.6 / 71.3 / 70.8 / 70.8 | 68.1 / 68.1 / 67.7 / 68.1 | ≈ −3.5 dB |
| st09 community | 60.2 / 58.5 / 58.0 / 56.8 | 52.6 / 53.0 / 52.1 / 51.6 | ≈ −6…−7 dB |

Useful order-of-magnitude facts, but **one hour per day — label as such, do
not present as a night average**. The robust measured contrast is
roadside-vs-community itself: live 24 h means 70.2–71.6 vs 56.4–59.0 dBA
(≈ 12–14 dB), and the annual exceedance rates 91–100 % vs 0–0.8 % of days.

## 5. Interpolation feasibility — reject

5 live points over 1,569 km² (≈ 1 per 300 km²), split across two exposure
classes that are *not* interchangeable (roadside vs community differ by
~12 dB by construction), with stale/dead stations and site-level effects
(school yards, housing compounds). Any kriging/IDW surface from this would
be fiction. **Use as point facts + as calibration/validation anchors for a
model, nothing finer.** Same verdict as any single-purpose sparse network;
if CRTKY-146's Air4Thai reaches a similar density decision, the two
networks can share one pipeline shape (facts at stations, model in between).

## 6. Modeled proxies for the night half (labeled `modeled`)

Everything needed is already in the pipeline's POI stock
(`research/bangkok/00-overview.md`):

- **Nightlife density** — the existing Nightlife category (Overture + OSM
  bars/pubs/clubs/live music/karaoke; strong/moderate/estimate per district)
  is the natural night-noise proxy; concentration is Sukhumvit/Thong Lo,
  Ratchada, RCA, Silom, Khaosan.
- **Major roads** — OSM `highway=motorway|trunk|primary` (+ secondary)
  distance/buffer, matching the roadside stations' exposure class; the
  measured anchor says roadside ≈ 70–75 dBA 24 h, i.e. above the 70 dBA
  standard essentially every day.
- **Population/places density** — current Quietness base (DOPA 2020 +
  Overture density, inverted).

Proposal shape (owner decides): keep district quietness as today for "day";
derive a **night quietness** = f(nightlife density, major-road proximity,
population density), tagged `modeled`/`estimate` confidence, calibrated by
sign against the 5 live stations (e.g. rank-correlation of modeled surface
vs station 24 h means + the 06:00-hour samples). District annotations where
a station exists: "PCD roadside monitor here: 70.6 dBA 24 h; over the 70
dBA standard 100 % of days in 2024" — measured facts, citable to the
annual report.

## 8. Composition recommendation (for the owner to decide)

1. **Adopt (measured, point level):** live daily 24 h Leq + annual
   mean/exceedance for the 5 live BKK stations → district **annotations**
   and validation anchors. Sources: noisemonitor/noise4thai portal (live)
   + PCD annual PDFs (2019–2024) + gdcatalog CSVs (2016–2025, Open Data
   Common) for history. Cite PCD, "preliminary daily data" caveat.
2. **Build (modeled):** night quietness proxy from nightlife + major roads
   + density; honest `modeled` label; day/night shown as two values or a
   delta chip on district pages ("quieter at night" / "loud late").
3. **Reject:** spatial interpolation of the network; treating the 06:00
   sample as "night noise"; the Woonkathi pilot as a source (offline).
4. **Keep on the backlog:** Traffy เสียงรบกวน (gated), PCD bulk export
   request (`noise@pcd.go.th`).
