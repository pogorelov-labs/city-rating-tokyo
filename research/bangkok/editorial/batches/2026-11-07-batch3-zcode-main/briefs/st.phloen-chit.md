## Guide lead (proposed)
Phloen Chit is old-money Bangkok in station form: BTS E2 on the Phloen Chit Road viaduct between Chit Lom and Nana, a skywalk straight into Central Embassy, 22 embassies down a tree-lined 2.6 km boulevard, and condo towers asking 25,000-90,000 THB a month. The ratings lean on quality more than quantity — a single top-ranked hotel bar for nightlife, a 2.2-ha heritage garden for green, one premium gym club for sport — while the unit's biggest changes (One Bangkok to the south, the old British Embassy site awaiting its confirmed successor) sit just outside the 800 m line or still on the drawing board.

## Strengths
- Skywalk-connected CBD node: the station enters Central Embassy without a street crossing, and Chit Lom's Ratchaprasong Sky Walk network (Central Chidlom skybridge, CentralWorld, Gaysorn) starts one stop west — 646 m away by coordinates. *[`phloen-chit-c03`, `phloen-chit-c01`]*
- The country's most expensive land signals real scarcity value: official 2023-2026 appraisals hit 1,000,000 THB/sq.wa on Witthayu, the British Embassy compound sold for a reported £420m, and condo resales set a reported 1,000,000 THB/sq.m record at 98 Wireless. *[`phloen-chit-c05`]*
- Quality-anchored nightlife: Lennon's at Rosewood — No. 7 on Asia's 50 Best Bars 2026 with the Highest New Entry award — plus Penthouse Bar + Grill at Park Hyatt and CHAR at Hotel Indigo, all in-unit. *[`phloen-chit-c04`]*
- A premium wellness layer: Virgin Active Wireless Road (75+ classes weekly) and the unusually shaded 2.6 km Witthayu boulevard for walking and road running. *[`phloen-chit-c13`]*

## Trade-offs

## Question coverage
- **Q01 — answered**: A single-line BTS strip (E2 Phloen Chit, opened 5 Dec 1999) on the elevated Phloen Chit Road viaduct, clipped by Chit Lom E1 ~646 m west and Nana E3 ~745 m east-southeast (haversine from infobox coordinates — both neighbors are inside the 800 m ring, so Voronoi clipping, not the ring, sets the strip). Inside: Central Embassy + Park Hyatt block (skywalk-connected), Rosewood, the Nai Lert Park Heritage garden behind it, Hotel Indigo and the lower Wireless Road embassy belt, Virgin Active Wireless Road, Mercury Ville. Outside: Lumphini Park (57.6 ha, south of Rama IV, ~1.1 km), One Bangkok (opened 25 Oct 2024 at Wireless x Rama IV, ~1.1 km, anchored on MRT Lumphini — the lumphini unit, not this one), Benchasiri Park (east, st.phrom-phong per app), Langsuan's dining row (toward chit-lom). Districts: core roads in Khwaeng Lumphini (Pathum Wan); Sukhumvit's north side in Khlong Toei Nuea (Watthana); a Ratchathewi sliver beyond the Saen Saep canal is consistent with khwaeng-boundary statements but shares are unverified. The station code is E2, not E1 as some older materials imply.
- **Q02 — answered**: A leafy, office-and-embassy CBD with a premium service layer: mornings on the shaded Witthayu boulevard, workdays on the skywalk between BTS and Central Embassy/Park Hyatt, lunches in mall food halls (Eathai) and hotel venues, evenings at hotel bars (Lennon's, Penthouse) — with Nana's tourist strip one stop east for louder nightlife. Daily life is expensive and internationally oriented; residential presence is thin district-wide (~18,000 people across all of Pathum Wan), so the street belongs to workers, shoppers and visitors more than to locals.
- **Q03 — answered**: Transport 10: a skywalk-connected CBD node on the city's backbone line with the Ratchaprasong Sky Walk one stop west, a khlong pier on Witthayu, and an expressway ramp — but single-line only, and the mechanism leans on skywalk quality and interchange proximity rather than in-unit network depth. Daily_essentials 10: mall-anchored premium groceries (Gourmet Market, Eathai, Mercury Ville) — convenience high, price tier premium.
