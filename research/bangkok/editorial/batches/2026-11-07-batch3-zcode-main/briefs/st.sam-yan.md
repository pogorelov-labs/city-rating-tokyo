## Card preview (proposed)
> A hospital-and-university junction on Rama IV where the campus landlord built the mall, the library runs 24 hours, and the nearest big park is a kilometre away in someone else's catchment.

## Guide lead (proposed)
Sam Yan is the junction where Rama IV Road meets Phaya Thai and Si Phraya under the Thai-Japan flyover — Chulalongkorn University land on the north side, Bang Rak's temples and shops across it. The 1.25 sq km catchment holds three institutions that never close: a 1,400-plus-bed teaching hospital east, a 41,000-student university north, and Samyan Mitrtown (2019), the mall the university's redevelopment wave produced, with its free 24-hour Samyan Co-op study floor and 24-hour retail zone. Days run on hospital shifts and semester calendars; nights run on the 24-hour layer rather than bars — the actual nightlife sits one MRT stop south in the Silom corridor. What you trade: the old Sam Yan of 1965 shophouses and the 1965 market was redeveloped out from 2008 onward (the displacement record is unusually well documented), the famous parks (Centenary, Lumpini) are both outside the boundary, and the district-blend rents overstate what a typical unit here actually asks.

## Strengths
- Institutional depth at the door: a top-tier teaching hospital (1,435-1,442 beds, 24h ER) ~0.6 km east and the country's oldest university (~41,600 students) on the north flank — constant demand keeps services dense. *[`sam-yan-c05`, `sam-yan-c06`]*
- The 24-hour layer is real infrastructure, not marketing: free 500-seat Samyan Co-op study space, a 24-hour retail zone, a 24-hour gym, all tunnel-connected to BL27 without crossing Rama IV. *[`sam-yan-c04`, `sam-yan-c03`]*
- Eating density matches the 462-venue count: a 1965 market lineage, hospital and campus canteens, and a Time Out 2020 coolest-neighbourhood crown built on the food-and-cafe mix. *[`sam-yan-c12`]*
- Car-free living works: the Blue Line loop station (3.54M passengers in 2021), underground mall links, buses at street level, and campus lanes north of the arterial. *[`sam-yan-c03`, `sam-yan-c06`]*

## Trade-offs
- The amenities were financed by displacement: the 2008 demolition plans, the market's relocation, ~200% Chamchuri vendor rent hikes, and the 2020 shrine eviction attempt are documented in news and activist sources — the neighbourhood's social cost is part of its physical form. *[`sam-yan-c09`, `sam-yan-c10`]*
- Green is campus-mediated: no single 3.2 ha public park exists inside the boundary — the green score rests on university gardens and stadium lawns, while Centenary Park (~1.0 km NW) and Lumpini (~1.0 km E) belong to other units. *[`sam-yan-c07`]*
- The nightlife-10 score outruns its footprint: 21 mapped venues against a documented character of food-and-study nights — the score prices proximity to Silom's corridor, not bars in the catchment. *[`sam-yan-c15`]*
- The rent blends mislead in both directions: newer Sam Yan stock asks ~20-40k (below the Bang Rak blend of ~71k, around the Pathum Wan blend's floor), and no signed-rent data exists; the old cheap stock is gone. *[`sam-yan-c14`, `sam-yan-c09`]*

## Day / night
- Morning-to-afternoon belongs to the hospital and campus: OPD queues, clinic runs, canteen meals, student flows on Phaya Thai — the weekday pulse of two institutions that never pause. *[`sam-yan-c05`, `sam-yan-c06`]*
- Evening shifts to Mitrtown's Eating Library and the tunnels; the junction's restaurants and the relocated market's stalls keep local hours. *[`sam-yan-c04`, `sam-yan-c12`]*
- Late night is a study-and-service night, not a bar night: the Co-op fills, the 24h zone and gym run, and the actual club traffic passes through the station toward Silom one stop south. *[`sam-yan-c04`, `sam-yan-c15`]*

## Within-area variation
- Junction core (NW/NE corners): Mitrtown and Chamchuri Square with the tunnels — retail, offices, the 24h layer; the most polished frontage. *[`sam-yan-c04`]*
- East (hospital block): 24/7 institutional rhythm — ambulances, visitors, pharmacies, medical-faculty courts; the densest foot traffic and the 2025 earthquake disruption. *[`sam-yan-c05`]*
- South and west (Si Phraya side and old lane fabric): Wat Hua Lamphong, shrines, the relocated market's direction, older shophouse remnants — the quietest corners and the closest thing to pre-redevelopment Sam Yan. *[`sam-yan-c08`, `sam-yan-c09`]*

## Conditional fit
- Choose Sam Yan if you are tied to the hospital or the university — staff, students, patients' families — or if a 24-hour study-and-gym rhythm matters more than a bar street; the tunnel-connected mall makes car-free daily life practical. *[`sam-yan-c04`, `sam-yan-c05`, `sam-yan-c06`]*
- Test the rent claim against real units: district blends (32.5k/71k) describe Pathum Wan and Bang Rak, not this stock; Chapter Chula-Samyan-style 1-beds ask ~20-40k, and older buildings ask less — verify per building before budgeting. *[`sam-yan-c14`]*
- Look elsewhere if you need big public green inside your daily radius, a within-catchment bar scene, or the old cheap-eats Sam Yan — all three live outside the boundary now. *[`sam-yan-c07`, `sam-yan-c15`, `sam-yan-c09`]*
