## Card preview (proposed)
> One khet, four khwaeng, the country's top land price and ten rail stations in 8.37 km2: Pathum Wan stacks five BTS stations, four MRT, a mainline terminus and ten boat piers onto 8.37 km2, wraps them around Lumphini Park's 57.6 ha — and prices every bit of it at rent 1.

## Guide lead (proposed)
Pathum Wan is Bangkok compressed into 8.37 km2: Siam Paragon's 400,000 m2 and Siam Square's 4,200 shops on Crown Property and university land, a 57.6-ha royal-donation park that opens at 04:30, the 36.3-ha Royal Bangkok Sports Club racetrack sitting members-only in the middle, Chulalongkorn University anchoring two khwaengs, and since 25 October 2024 One Bangkok's THB-120bn district at Wireless x Rama IV with its own park and an underground MRT link. The registered population keeps shrinking (139,008 in 1992 to 40,077 in 2023) while footfall counters spin — Siam Paragon alone reports 150,000-200,000 visitors a day, and every New Year's Eve the police close six roads around Ratchaprasong from noon. Rents lead the city: district-mean USD23/sqm, top appraisals 1,000,000 THB per sq wa, and the blend of 38,000/85,000 THB maps cleanly onto the district's 1BR/2BR asking percentiles.

## Strengths
- Ten stations inside one district: BTS Siam interchange plus Chit Lom, Phloen Chit, Ratchadamri and National Stadium; MRT Lumphini, Sam Yan, Hua Lamphong and Si Lom; the SRT terminus — ten stations inside one district, plus ten boat piers on two canals. *[`pathum-wan-c05`, `pathum-wan-c06`]*
- Two major hospitals and a premium everyday layer in-khet: King Chulalongkorn Memorial (1,442 beds, Thai Red Cross, CU teaching hospital) and Police General (650 beds) on the same footprint as the city's newest 4,600-m2 supermarket. *[`pathum-wan-c10`]*
- Lumphini Park is genuinely inside the polygon: 57.6 ha, 04:30-21:00 daily, a 2.5 km running loop and BMA operation, plus the 4.5-ha Centenary Park and One Bangkok's 8 ha of on-site greenery. *[`pathum-wan-c08`]*
- One Bangkok materially upgraded the southern khwaeng from 25 Oct 2024: a 17-ha mixed district with three office towers trading, a 1.5 km Food Loop, and a direct underground walk from MRT Lumphini. *[`pathum-wan-c04`]*

## Trade-offs
- Rent 1 is land-priced, not hype: 1,000,000 THB/sq wa appraisals on Phloen Chit, Witthayu and Rama I (country max), market trades to 3.9M, district-mean rent 28.5% above Bangkok — and the flagship towers price 9.1% above the district mean itself. *[`pathum-wan-c07`]*
- About a third of the mapped green is not yours to use: the 36.3-ha Royal Bangkok Sports Club is members-only (grandstand on race Sundays excepted) and Sa Pathum Palace's 7.9-ha garden is royal ground. *[`pathum-wan-c08`, `pathum-wan-c09`]*
- Crowd 1 is visitor arithmetic: 150-200k/day through Siam Paragon, ~400k/day through Siam Square, six roads closed for NYE — against roughly 40,000 registered residents, down from 139,008 in 1992. *[`pathum-wan-c11`, `pathum-wan-c02`]*
- The Siam/Ratchaprasong core runs on mall hours and closed streets; ground-level street life between the megablocks is arterial traffic, and the district's mainline rail history left for Krung Thep Aphiwat in 2023. *[`pathum-wan-c13`, `pathum-wan-c14`]*

## Day / night
- Morning: Lumphini opens 04:30 for runners, Saen Saep boats from 05:30 weekdays, office towers fill the Witthayu-Phloen Chit belt; the residential Rong Mueang side stays quiet. *[`pathum-wan-c12`, `pathum-wan-c06`]*
- Afternoon: tutoring-school students flood Siam Square toward evening; campus life dominates Wang Mai; One Bangkok's towers hold a footfall split the operator puts at precisely 50/50 local-vs-destination. *[`pathum-wan-c12`, `pathum-wan-c03`, `pathum-wan-c04`]*
- Evening: mall hours to ~22:00 carry the food and nightlife load (3,064/164 app counts); Lumphini is already closed at 21:00 — night green is private-estate only. *[`pathum-wan-c09`, `pathum-wan-c12`]*
- Weekend/event: retail peaks (festive projections 400-450k/day group-wide), race Sundays every other week at RBSC, and NYE brings six-road closures from noon to 05:00. *[`pathum-wan-c11`, `pathum-wan-c08`]*

## Within-area variation
- Khwaeng Lumphini (3.2-3.5 km2, 17,040 residents): the Ploenchit-Chidlom-Ratchadamri-Witthayu premium belt, Lumphini Park, and now One Bangkok — highest land prices in the country. *[`pathum-wan-c01`, `pathum-wan-c07`, `pathum-wan-c04`]*
- Khwaeng Pathum Wan (2.2-2.3 km2, 2,746 residents): Siam Paragon, Siam Square, CentralWorld, Erawan Shrine, Police General Hospital, Suphachalasai Stadium — the visitor machine with almost nobody registered. *[`pathum-wan-c01`, `pathum-wan-c03`]*
- Khwaeng Wang Mai (1.4 km2, 5,093 residents): the Chula campus, MBK, Jim Thompson House, Sam Yan's MRT node and Chulalongkorn Memorial Hospital — the student/hospital quarter. *[`pathum-wan-c01`, `pathum-wan-c10`]*
- Khwaeng Rong Mueang (1.3-1.4 km2, 15,198 residents): Hua Lamphong station (SRT+MRT) and the district office — the most residential khwaeng, absorbing the district's mainline-rail loss. *[`pathum-wan-c01`, `pathum-wan-c14`, `pathum-wan-c02`]*

## Conditional fit
- Premium seekers get the strongest address argument in Bangkok — top national land appraisals, flagship towers, a 57.6-ha park next door — if the asking percentiles fit. *[`pathum-wan-c07`, `pathum-wan-c08`]*
- Car-free households are the clearest winners: ten stations, two canal boat lines, event-grade bus access, and a police-endorsed walk-the-skywalk culture on closure days. *[`pathum-wan-c05`, `pathum-wan-c06`, `pathum-wan-c13`]*
- Students and academics: the Chula campus spans two khwaengs with Centenary Park, Sam Yan MRT and the tutoring capital within one district — but student-housing rents sit on the same expensive land. *[`pathum-wan-c03`, `pathum-wan-c12`, `pathum-wan-c07`]*
- Poor fits: budget renters (1BR district percentile ~USD1,040/month), quiet-seekers near the retail core on weekends and event days, drivers during closures, and late-evening green users (park closes 21:00). *[`pathum-wan-c15`, `pathum-wan-c11`, `pathum-wan-c09`]*

## Question coverage
- **Q01 — answered**: The whole khet Pathum Wan (เขตปทุมวัน): 8.369 km2, four khwaengs (Rong Mueang, Wang Mai, Pathum Wan, Lumphini), bounded by Khlong Saen Saep/Mahanak (N), the SRT Maenam railway (E), Rama IV (S) and Khlong Phadung Krung Kasem (W) — no river frontage. INSIDE (PIP-verified): Siam Paragon, CentralWorld, MBK (Wang Mai), Siam Square, One Bangkok (khwaeng Lumphini — the batch-2 'which khet?' question resolves to pathum-wan), Lumphini Park, RBSC, Chula campus and Centenary Park, Jim Thompson House (Wang Mai), Erawan Shrine, King Chulalongkorn Memorial + Police General hospitals, and SRT+MRT Hua Lamphong in Rong Mueang (the app's Hua Lamphong alias is correct). OUTSIDE: BNH Hospital (Bang Rak), BTS Sala Daeng (Bang Rak), ARL stations (Ratchathewi), Wat Hua Lamphong temple (Bang Rak's khwaeng Si Phraya — note the temple is outside while the STATION is inside). The app center falls in khwaeng Lumphini.
- **Q02 — answered**: Three overlapping daily lives: (1) the retail core — Siam/Ratchaprasong runs on mall hours and visitor flow (Paragon alone 150-200k/day) with tutoring-school students filling Siam Square's afternoons; (2) the office/embassy belt — Witthayu-Phloen Chit-Chit Lom weekdays, extended since Oct 2024 by One Bangkok's office towers and its '50/50' local-vs-destination footfall; (3) the residential/university interior — Rong Mueang and Wang Mai hold most of the ~40k registered residents around Hua Lamphong, Sam Yan and the Chula campus, where registered population has fallen continuously since 1992. The khet is a daytime/weekend magnet on a small, depopulating residential base.
- **Q03 — answered**: Transport 10 rewards verifiable saturation: 10 rail stations (5 BTS incl the Siam interchange, 4 MRT, 1 SRT) in 8.37 km2, plus ~10 boat piers on two canals and the event-grade bus layer. Daily-essentials 10 rewards two large hospitals (Chulalongkorn Memorial 1,442 beds; Police General 650) and a premium retail/supermarket layer (Paragon dept store 50,000 m2; Mitsukoshi Depachika 4,600 m2). Food 10 / nightlife 10 map the 3,064/164 venue counts onto the densest mall-dining precinct in the country (One Bangkok's 1.5 km Food Loop alone adds ~250). Green 10 rewards Lumphini's 57.6 ha + campus/Centenary/One-Bangkok green — though ~36 ha of the mapped green is members-only (c08/c09). Vibe 9 and crowd 1 are two readings of the same mechanism: extraordinary visitor gravity on a small residential base.
- **Q04 — answered**: Rent 1 is the honest floor — and it is land-priced: 1,000,000 THB/sq wa appraisals (country max) on three khet roads, market trades 2-3.9M; Hipflat district rent USD23/sqm (+28.5% vs city) with a 1BR percentile ~USD1,040. The +9.1% finding from batch 2 does NOT soften it — Noble Ploenchit prices 9.1% ABOVE the district mean. Complications: crowd 1 is visitor-driven and clock-dependent (residential base ~40k and shrinking); green 10 overstates public access (RBSC 36.3 ha members-only, Sa Pathum palace garden royal, Lumphini closes 21:00); transport 10 lost its long-distance rail role in 2023 (Hua Lamphong); safety 7 has no district-specific crime source found here (out-of-scope beyond anchors); and the daily-essentials premium means the ordinary wet-market layer the rest of the city relies on is thin in the mapped data.
- **Q05 — answered**: Clock: Lumphini opens 04:30 (runners before dawn), boats 05:30 weekdays, offices fill Witthayu/One Bangkok towers by 09:00, tutoring/after-school flow peaks late afternoon in Siam Square, malls own 10:00-22:00, and event nights (NYE, Songkran) bring police closures to Rama I/Ratchadamri/Phloen Chit from midday. Week: weekdays are office+student days; weekends are the retail peak (festive projections 400-450k/day group-wide) and race Sundays open RBSC's grandstand to the public every other week. Year: Chula semester swings unverifiable; festive-season spikes documented for Oct-Dec.
- **Q06 — answered**: The district walks on two levels. Elevated/underground: the National Stadium-Siam-Chit Lom skywalk chain links Paragon/MBK/CentralWorld; the Ratchaprasong R-Walk extends through Chit Lom/Gaysorn; One Bangkok connects underground from MRT Lumphini into its B1; MBK-Chula footbridges cross Phaya Thai Rd. Ground level: wide arterials (Rama I, Rama IV, Ratchadamri, Witthayu) with event-day closures proving the roads are car-hostile at peak — police route pedestrians via station exits and the boat pier. The southern residential half (Rong Mueang/Wang Mai) is low-rise and walkable in genre terms but was not sourced this round. No shade/heat experiential evidence found.
- **Q07 — answered**: Hospitals: two majors in-khet — King Chulalongkorn Memorial (Thai Red Cross, 1,442/1,435 beds, CU teaching hospital, at Sam Yan on Rama IV) and Police General (650 beds, Rama I Rd, khwaeng Pathum Wan); plus Chula's dentistry hospital and Jetanin. BNH is across Rama IV in Bang Rak — the app note questioning its placement resolves to outside. Schools: Chulalongkorn University dominates two khwaengs; 30+ tutoring schools make Siam Square the country's tutoring center. Groceries: premium-skewed — Paragon Department Store (50,000 m2), MITSUKOSHI DEPACHIKA (4,600 m2) at One Bangkok; no large wet market identified in OSM inside the khet (an absence finding, not a census). District office sits in Rong Mueang.
- **Q08 — answered**: The app blend 38,000/85,000 THB tracks the independent district evidence: Hipflat's Pathum Wan percentiles are USD1,040 (1BR) and USD2,348 (2BR) per month — about 37k/84k THB at 35-36 THB/USD (researcher conversion). So the blend is a district-mean 1BR/2BR figure, and rent 1 is consistent with it: the district mean is 28.5% above Bangkok's, the three top land appraisals in the country sit here, and the flagship Ploenchit towers price ANOTHER 9.1% above the district mean (Noble Ploenchit USD25 vs USD23/sqm — the batch-2 tension resolved: unit premium vs district mean, not district cheapness). Intra-district spread is real (older Rong Mueang/Wang Mai stock below the mean) but the mapped asking floor in the premium pocket was still ~13-18k THB for 24 m2 studios in sibling research. NOTE for the coordinator: a sibling file describes the district blend as '33,500/74,000' — the two figures should be reconciled (OI-1); this file does not change app data.
- **Q09 — answered**: Mostly, with one honest caveat. The khet genuinely contains Lumphini Park (57.6 ha official / 52.3 OSM, 04:30-21:00 daily, BMA-run) fully inside khwaeng Lumphini — the largest central public park — plus the 4.5-ha Chula Centenary Park (2017 flood-sponge design), One Bangkok's 8 ha of on-site greenery (OSM-named park 1.1 ha), and campus greenery. But the app's 102.7 ha includes ~36.3 ha of members-only RBSC (golf+racetrack) and the 7.9-ha royal Sa Pathum garden: roughly 60-65% of the mapped green is publicly usable. Quiet pockets exist (park interior, campus lanes, Rong Mueang side streets) but no source quantifies quiet; the Siam/Ratchaprasong side is the loudest precinct in the city by footfall.
- **Q10 — answered**: PICK IT: premium-seekers who accept the city's top asking rents for walk-to-everything CBD access; car-free households (10 stations + boats + buses); students/academics (Chula + tutoring ecosystem); park-lovers who want a 57.6-ha park at their khwaeng; culture-listers (Jim Thompson House, Erawan Shrine, Wat Pathumwanaram, BACC). SKIP IT: budget renters (1BR percentile ~37k THB; no cheap-stock pocket in the data); quiet-dependent residents near the retail core on weekends/event days; drivers on event days (6-road closures); anyone needing late-evening public green (21:00 close) or wet-market-priced groceries.
- **Q11 — answered**: 25 Oct 2024 — One Bangkok opens (Parade + The Storeys retail, three office towers already live; Ritz-Carlton targeted Nov 2024, Andaz 2025; other reporting dates the actual openings Dec 2024 and 19 Dec 2025 — conflicted, OI-4). Through 2026 — POST 1928 retail (40,000 m2) still unopened; Sep 2026: occupancy ~96->98%, 40M+ visits/year claimed, second office tower + residence + two hotels slated by 2027. 2021-2023 rolling into this window — Scala cinema demolished by Chula (2021), site empty as of 2023; Hua Lamphong lost long-distance rail (19 Jan 2023) and pivots to cultural use toward its 2026 110th anniversary. Annually — Ratchaprasong NYE closures (Dec 2025: 6 roads, noon-to-05:00). Watch: next Treasury appraisal review could move Witthayu/Ploenchit from 1M toward 2M THB/sq wa.
- **Q12 — answered**: Outside the score: the daytime population gravity (hundreds of thousands of visitors on a ~40k residential base) that the ratings monetize but never count; RBSC's ~36 ha of members-only green and the royal palace garden; the unpriced cost of event-day road closures for residents; Hua Lamphong's final redevelopment direction. Uncertainties: hotel opening status at One Bangkok (conflicted sources); One Bangkok site size (104 vs 108 rai); the app's temple count (55) vs 6 named wats in OSM (category definition unknown); app rent-blend value differing between sibling files (38k/85k here vs 33.5k/74k quoted there); weekend-specific crowd counts (none published); heat/shade walking experience (no source); whether Pathum Wan's USD23/sqm mean is the highest of all 50 khet (not checked district-by-district).

## Claim index

- `pathum-wan-c01` **[corroborated·neutral·entire_unit]** Khet Pathum Wan is Bangkok's central district: 8.369 km2 (both Wikipedias), divided into FOUR khwaeng — Rong Mueang, Wang Mai, Pathum Wan, Lumphini (EN wiki areas 1.423/1.423/2.276/3.247 km2; Thai wiki 1.300/1.403/2.181/3.485 km2; OSM shoelace 1.28-1.37/2.12/3.39 km2 — three datasets disagree at khwaeng level, khet total agrees). Established as an amphoe in 1915, a khet since 1972; named after King Mongkut's royal villa 'lotus forest' (paduma+vana) beside Wat Pathumwanaram.
