## Guide lead (proposed)
Soi Sena Ruam — officially Phahon Yothin Soi 11 — is a residential side-soi grid in Phaya Thai strung along the Phahonyothin corridor: two private hospitals and a compound park at the edges, Villa Market and La Villa within 700 m, craft bars spilling in from the Ari end, and a BTS station that has been 'coming' since 1999 and still adds 25 baht to the fare table in 2026.
