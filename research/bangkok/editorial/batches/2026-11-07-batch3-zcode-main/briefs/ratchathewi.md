## Card preview (proposed)
> A four-station khet stitched from Pratunam's market economy, pocket parks no bigger than 3.4 ha, and corridors that currently wear an Orange Line construction site.

## Guide lead (proposed)
Ratchathewi packs four researched station areas (BTS Ratchathewi, ARL Ratchaprarop, BTS/ARL Phaya Thai, Victory Monument) into 7.1 km² with four khwaeng. Its green 9 rests on pocket parks — Phirom 3.38 ha, Santiphap 2.85 ha, Suan Rom 1.71 ha — plus institutional gardens; no public park above ~3.4 ha exists anywhere in the khet. Pratunam's wholesale economy and Baiyoke towers (khwaeng Thanon Phaya Thai) anchor the east, and the MRT Orange Line (three in-khet stations, opening ~July 2030) is reshaping its main corridors. Safety 5 repeats the pattern seen at station level: no per-khet statistics exist in any retrieved source.

## Strengths
- District-level transit is structurally rich: three BTS stations, ARL and the under-construction Orange Line (OR08 Ratchathewi, OR09 Pratunam, OR10 Ratchaprarop). *[`ratchathewi-d-c03`]*
- Rent 7 is bracketed by live asking data (FazWaz 1,864 listings, 13,000-300,000 THB). *[`ratchathewi-d-c15`]*

## Trade-offs
- Green 9 decomposes to 8.81 ha of named public parks plus small institutional/private garden stock — pocket-scale, not park-scale. *[`ratchathewi-d-c06`]*
- The Ratchathewi flyover is closed (since mid-Jan 2025) until 30 Apr 2028, and the Pratunam overpass demolition window is contested between sources. *[`ratchathewi-d-c12`]*

## Day / night
- Pratunam's wholesale day economy and the office corridors set weekday rhythm; Phetchaburi Road runs quieter at night. *[`ratchathewi-d-c09`]*

## Within-area variation
- Pratunam/Makkasan market-and-tower blocks, Thanon Phaya Thai's towers, and the Rangnam/Thanon Phetchaburi mid-rise belt are distinct subareas. *[`ratchathewi-d-c01`]*

## Conditional fit
- Strong fit: market traders, airport commuters and transit-first renters who accept pocket-park green and construction disruption. *[`ratchathewi-d-c16`]*

## Explicit unknowns
- rating transport (9): Defensible as district-level modal redundancy: three BTS stations, both in-khet ARL stops, an SRT halt, canal boats on the whole southern edge, and three Orange Line stations under construction including two interchanges. The caveat is timing - the BTS/MRT interchange at Ratchathewi and ARL/MRT at Ratchaprarop open ~2030, so today's 9 describes the stack, not interchange quality (batch-2/3's headway findings carry over).
- rating rent (7): The 18,000/38,000 blend sits inside FazWaz's live spread (13,000-300,000, typical 1-BR 20,000-28,000; 38,000 = 2024-vintage stock). Rent 7 = moderate affordability held by older stock and townhouses while new supply lands at the band's top - matches batch-2's PropertyScout/Superagent ranges.
- rating daily_essentials (9): The strongest-evidenced score at district level: six public hospitals + two private, a university cluster, the Wannasorn tutoring hub, BMA cultural facilities and the market/convenience substrate. Mechanism is institutional depth, not mall coverage.
- rating safety (5): Fifth entity tested, same result: no district-level crime statistics exist in open sources; the only quantitative comparator is citywide and unstable (study revision moved Bangkok from reported #1 to #23). Pratunam scam lore is corridor perception, not a district pattern. The 5 remains an untested prior - flag stays open.
- rating food (9): Two one-star Michelin venues (not one) plus the wholesale-belt food economy and canal-side community eating (batch-2's Soi 5/Soi 10 streets). The app's 1,116-food-venue count reflects a richer POI database than OSM (12 worship vs 60 'temples' shows the same DB gap).
- rating green (9): The 17.8 ha figure now decomposes honestly: 9.25 ha parks + 8.48 ha gardens on OSM, but only 8.81 ha is named public park, largest single park 3.38 ha (Phirom). Green 9 is carried by pocket + institutional green; park-scale quiet does not exist in the khet. The score survives only because the stock is dense and close, not because it is large.
- rating gym_sports (9): Least-evidenced score, as at station level: OSM shows 10+ pitches in the khet (8 tennis, 2 basketball, 2 soccer, futsal/volleyball/sepak-takraw, multi) plus hotel/hospital facilities; no district-level gym inventory was retrieved. 9 is plausible for a district this dense but rests on thin sourcing.
- rating vibe (9): The district's identity is genuinely layered: wholesale Pratunam, the Baiyoke skyline, the 1942 Victory Monument roundabout, Bangkok's oldest Muslim community weaving silk by the canal, the Phaya Thai Palace belt. The 9 is defensible as layered character, with the crowd cost scored separately.
- rating nightlife (9): Anchors: Baiyoke Sky's 77F-84F observatory/bar/revolving deck, the hotel-bar/karaoke stock of the Pratunam belt (batch-3: OSM tags zero clubs in its circle while the app counts venues), Century Movie Plaza still screening near Victory Monument. Nightlife here is hotel- and screen-led, not club-led - the 9 leans on breadth of venues the app's DB sees and OSM does not.
- rating crowd (2): Consistently supported: the dawn wholesale cycle puts porter and vendor traffic on the streets before 05:00, the Pratunam belt is the documented crush zone, the Victory Monument roundabout is a city bus hub, and 2025-2028 construction adds work crews. Nothing retrieved offsets the 2.

## Question coverage
- **Q01 — answered**: Four khwaeng verified three ways (EN wiki, TH wiki, OSM partition summing exactly to the khet): Thung Phaya Thai (seat), Thanon Phaya Thai, Thanon Phetchaburi, Makkasan. - **Q02 — answered**: Four distinguishable subareas: the Pratunam wholesale belt (1961 produce market to garment hub; dawn trade 04:00-09:00 per batch-3's Wongnai read); Makkasan khwaeng - SRT-land railway communities and the Bueng Makkasan pit (644 households relocated to its edge, CODI); canal-side Ban Khrua - Bangkok's oldest Muslim community with silk workshops and three mosques; the northern hospital/education/palace belt in Thung Phaya Thai. No single 'district experience' survives these differences.

- **Q04 — answered**: Safety 5 at district level has no statistical basis in any retrieved source - the same absence-of-evidence pattern as the four station-area entities; only corridor-level tourist-scam perception around Pratunam exists (and even the Pratunam wiki article carries no safety content).
