## Guide lead (proposed)
Sam Yot is the old town's quiet rail corner — an underground Blue-line station beneath Charoen Krung at the Sam Yot junction, straddling Phra Nakhon, Pom Prap Sattru Phai and Samphanthawong. The Khlong Ong Ang promenade (Friday-Sunday walking street, kayaks, street art) runs beside the station, and listings put 11,000-13,000 THB studios and 14,000-15,000 THB 1-bedrooms at the door. The trade-offs: safety 5 has no visible unit-specific basis, evening life is event-driven rather than street-level, and the district's registered population is shrinking. Purple Line interchange targeted November 2030.

- `sam-yot-c04` **[corroborated·neutral·inside]** The name comes from a Grand Palace outer gate. A wooden gate (Phueat Thibat, ประตูพฤฒิบาศ) stood there from Rama I's reign; under Rama V it was replaced by a cement gate with three gateways, each topped by a spire — hence 'Sam Yot', literally three peaks.
