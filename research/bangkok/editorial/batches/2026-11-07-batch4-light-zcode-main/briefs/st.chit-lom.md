## Card preview (proposed)
> Chit Lom is Ratchaprasong at its most valuable: the Erawan Shrine corner, a skywalk spine into CentralWorld and Central Chidlom, canal boats from Pratunam - and the app's highest rent figure (45,000/120,000), which the market data says is a top-decile luxury number, not a median one.

## Guide lead (proposed)
One stop east of Siam, Chit Lom swaps the student square for shrine incense and office-tower lobbies: E1 on the Sukhumvit line, wired by skywalk to CentralWorld, Gaysorn and Central Chidlom, with Khlong Saen Saep boats at Pratunam and Chidlom piers. Its transport, food and daily-essentials scores rival Siam's; its rent record - a 45,000/120,000 station override dated 2026-10-21 - is the steepest in the app, and the listings show exactly who pays it.

## Strengths
- A walkable retail-and-shrine core: E1 station with a direct skybridge into Central Chidlom and a Sky Walk running through Ratchaprasong to Siam, with two canal-boat piers (Pratunam interchange, Chidlom) adding a water route the malls cannot. *[`chit-lom-c01`, `chit-lom-c04`]*
- Food and nightlife depth (624 food, 83 cafes, 39 nightlife per app) anchored by CentralWorld-scale retail and the shrine's all-day draw. *[`chit-lom-c02`, `chit-lom-c10`]*

## Trade-offs
- The 120,000 rent headline is a top-decile luxury figure: corridor medians run near 40,000 and the FazWaz 2-bed average is 99,600 - rent=1 is earned by the district, but the override overstates the typical renter's price point by about a third. *[`chit-lom-c09`, `chit-lom-c07`]*
- Security history is heavy for a shopping district: the 2015 Erawan Shrine bombing (20 killed, 125 injured) and the 2010 CentralWorld arson sit inside the unit's memory, qualifying safety=7. *[`chit-lom-c03`]*
- Green=9 rests on unverified composite polygons - no flagship public park is confirmed inside the unit. *[`chit-lom-c05`]*

## Day / night
- Weekdays belong to the office towers and lunch queues; early evening shifts to the shrine (guide-era window 06:00-midnight) and the food strip; boats stop at 20:30 weekdays and 19:00 weekends; the mall system closes around 22:00 and the unit's residential streets go quiet with it. *[`chit-lom-c10`, `chit-lom-c02`]*

## Within-area variation
- The skywalk-mall layer around the station is a different world from the canal-side blocks 600 m north (pier lanes, older low-rise) and from the green ambiguity at the unit's southern edge toward Lumphini's northern lobe - three textures the single 4.2 ha park figure flattens. *[`chit-lom-c04`, `chit-lom-c05`]*

## Conditional fit
- Fit for corporate and hospitality workers, shrine-adjacent lifestyle renters, and luxury-budget households who want two rail lines' reach without Siam's crush. *[`chit-lom-c07`, `chit-lom-c01`]*
- Poor fit for typical-budget renters: if 45,000-120,000 reads as the market, the 40,000-100,000 reality of most corridor stock still prices out the Bangkok median - and crowd=1 weekends at the shrine-mall corner are part of the deal. *[`chit-lom-c09`, `chit-lom-c03`]*

## Explicit unknowns

- Pier count 5 = 4 duplicate Pratunam OSM nodes + 1 Chidlom node; 2 distinct stops served (chit-lom-c04).
- 4.2 ha park composition unresolved; unnamed OSM polygons unverified (chit-lom-c05).
- Aman Nai Lert's cell assignment (chit-lom vs phloen-chit Voronoi) unknown; opening date snippet-level only.
- Dispatch brief's '@13.7385' coordinate contradicts batch.json and Wikipedia (13.7441); app data internally consistent.

## Claim index

- `chit-lom-c01` **[corroborated·strength·entire_unit]** BTS Chit Lom (code E1, Sukhumvit line) opened 5 December 1999 at 13.7441083 N, 100.5430972 E - identical to the app's unit center. A Sky Walk links the station to the Ratchaprasong intersection (CentralWorld, Gaysorn Plaza, Erawan Shrine) and onward to Siam station; Central Chidlom department store has a direct skybridge from the station. The dispatch brief's '@13.7385-ish' coordinate is contradicted by both batch.json and Wikipedia; 13.7385 sits near Phloen Chit station, not Chit Lom. _(scope: station_area — BTS Chit Lom station at Ratchaprasong, Pathum Wan)_
- `chit-lom-c02` **[proposed·neutral·inside]** The unit's anchor is the Ratchaprasong intersection: the Erawan Shrine (formally Thao Maha Phrom), whose Phra Phrom statue was enshrined on 9 November 1956 after a string of mishaps during the Erawan Hotel's construction (wrong foundation date, cost overruns, injuries, a lost marble shipment), stands beside the Grand Hyatt Erawan; CentralWorld - one of the world's largest malls, served by both Chit Lom and Siam stations - fronts the same junction. _(scope: station_area — Ratchaprasong intersection)_
- `chit-lom-c03` **[proposed·tradeoff·inside]** The area's defining security event: on 17 August 2015 at 18:55 a 3 kg TNT pipe bomb detonated near the Erawan Shrine, killing 20 bystanders and injuring 125. Earlier, the 2010 political confrontation damaged CentralWorld itself - arson closed the mall on 19 May 2010, with most of it reopening 28 September 2010 and the Zen wing only in January 2012. _(scope: station_area — Erawan Shrine; CentralWorld)_
- `chit-lom-c04` **[corroborated·neutral·inside]** Piers: the app counts 5, but OSM resolves this to 2 distinct Khlong Saen Saep boat stops inside the unit - Pratunam (the Golden Mount line's western terminus/interchange; 4 duplicate ferry_terminal nodes at 595-621 m) and Chidlom (ferry_terminal node at 537 m plus a pier way at 523 m).
