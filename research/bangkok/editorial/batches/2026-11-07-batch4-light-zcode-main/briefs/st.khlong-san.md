## Card preview (proposed)
> A west-bank residential district with a 2020 people-mover stop, one hop from the BTS, facing the city's most ambitious riverfront mall across 500 metres of Charoen Nakhon Road.

## Guide lead (proposed)
Khlong San station ends the BTS Gold Line on Somdet Chao Phraya Road, deep in a low-rise, temple-dotted west-bank district — with ICONSIAM's riverfront a short hop down the line at Charoen Nakhon station and the BTS Silom Line one stop beyond that.

## Strengths
- A rare combination for the price band: residential west-bank quiet with a direct people-mover link to the BTS Silom Line and river piers for cross-river life. *[`khlong-san-st-c11`, `khlong-san-st-c07`]*
- ICONSIAM's retail and river park sit just off the unit's edge, giving mall-grade daily life without a mall-grade address premium. *[`khlong-san-st-c06`]*

## Trade-offs
- Public green is nearly absent inside the unit (0.2 ha) — the big parks named for the district sit beyond the catchment and the riverside park is private. *[`khlong-san-st-c08`]*
- The Gold Line is a light people-mover: ~2.8 million riders a year system-wide, service to midnight, and a 2026 ridership slide — not a trunk line. *[`khlong-san-st-c04`, `khlong-san-st-c03`]*

- **Q01 — answered**: The BTS Gold Line G3 terminus in front of Taksin Hospital — a 2020 people-mover stop, not an MRT stop, and not the ICONSIAM stop (that is G2, ~500 m away); catchment 83.9% Khlong San district plus a Samphanthawong sliver over the river.
- **Q02 — answered**: A residential west-bank district stitched to a mega-mall riverfront: temples and low-rise sois inland, ICONSIAM's River Park and decks ~500 m away, commute via a one-stop people-mover hop to the BTS.
- **Q03 — answered**: High transport and vibe scores ride on the Gold Line-to-BTS hop and the riverfront's gravity; food 9 leans on the mall and district sois; the west-bank address buys river proximity at below-riverside rents.
- **Q04 — answered**: Green 5 (0.2 ha) is genuinely low; nightlife 9 against 10 venues looks edge-dependent; Gold Line ridership is small and falling through 2026.
- **Q05 — answered**: Morning and evening commute pulses through G3 and Krung Thon Buri; mall evenings and weekend riverfront crowds; residential quiet otherwise; everything winds down by midnight with the line.
- **Q06 — answered**: Flat west-bank grid with the station at Taksin Hospital's door and Somdet Chao Phraya Road frontage; no walking durations derived from catchment radius in this bundle.
- **Q07 — answered**: Daily needs run through district sois (65 essentials POIs per app) plus the mall; 11 temples and riverside heritage shape the backdrop; piers give river access.
- **Q08 — answered**: App 19,000/42,500 sits below PropertyScout's luxury-skewed listing means (1-bed ~27-28k) — consistent with non-riverside stock and budget pockets (7-10k reported in Khlong Ton Sai); the 2-bed 42,500 is far under the riverside-skewed mean, implying a mid-market blend.
- **Q09 — answered**: The unit's own green is a 0.2-ha remnant; the district's named parks and ICONSIAM's River Park sit outside or at the edge — green 5 is honest, quiet is real inland of the riverfront.
- **Q10 — answered**: Strong fit for renters who want a residential address with riverfront weekends and a one-hop BTS commute; weak fit for park users, late-night dwellers, or anyone requiring frequent rapid transit (a 2.77M/year people mover).
- **Q11 — answered**: Free-fare week (Jan 2025), earthquake suspension (Mar 2025), declining 2026 ridership, ICONSIAM ICS/hotel addition (2023) — but no confirmed Phase 2 or new rail infrastructure in body-read sources.
- **Q12 — answered**: Unresolved: identity of the 0.2-ha park object, which two piers the app counts, ICONSIAM's edge-membership in this unit, the fare's 15-vs-16 discrepancy, and peak train frequency (summary-only); anchors verified clean.
