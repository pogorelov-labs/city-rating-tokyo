## Card preview (proposed)
> Old Thonburi living at the old prices: a century-old market street at Talat Phlu, three BTS stations, King Taksin's riverfront heritage - and almost no public park anywhere in the khet.

## Guide lead (proposed)
Thon Buri khet is west-bank family Bangkok at its most affordable: seven khwaeng of markets, temples and low-rise streets between the Chao Phraya and the khlongs, served by BTS Pho Nimit, Talat Phlu and Wutthakat plus the Maeklong-line terminal at Wongwian Yai. Registered population has fallen 57% since 1992 to 98,286, rents run from about 11,500 THB, and the food story is Talat Phlu's decades-old shops - nationally famous again after the 2024 film. The trade-offs are structural: the batch's lowest green (4.6 ha, 0.6%), no full-service hospital inside the khet, and the BTS Wongwian Yai terminus itself sitting across the Khlong San line.

## Strengths
- Cheapest of the batch with real transit: 1-bed portal averages ~USD 387 (13-14k THB), three in-polygon BTS Silom stations, the Maeklong terminal and the west bank's biggest bus hub. *[`thon-buri-c05`, `thon-buri-c08`, `thon-buri-c03`]*
- Authentic old-Bangkok texture: Talat Phlu's 80-year dessert shop and 60-year khao mu dang, the Kudi Chin riverfront and the new Memorial Bridge-to-Wat Kalayanamit walkway. *[`thon-buri-c07`, `thon-buri-c12`]*

## Trade-offs
- Green 4 is the honest floor: no district-scale park inside the khet - the main named green is a ~10-rai pocket park, and the riverside showpiece (Chao Phraya Sky Park) is outside the polygon. *[`thon-buri-c06`]*
- No full-service hospital verified inside the khet: Siriraj is Bangkok Noi across the canal, and the wikis' Somdech Phra Pinklao entry falls outside the app polygon too. *[`thon-buri-c09`]*

- **Q01 — answered**: Khet Thon Buri, 8.551 km2, seven khwaeng (Wat Kanlaya, Hiran Ruchi, Bang Yi Ruea, Bukkhalo, Talat Phlu, Dao Khanong, Samre), bounded by Khlong Bangkok Yai + river (N), Khlong Bang Sai Kai/Taksin/Prajadhipok roads (E vs Khlong San), river + Khlong Dao Khanong (S), khlongs (W vs Chom Thong/Phasi Charoen). - **Q02 — answered**: A low-rise family khet of markets, temples and schools strung along the BTS Silom band: Talat Phlu's century market street, Bukkhalo and Samre neighbourhoods, Wongwian Yai's bus-rail junction; the riverfront Wat Kanlaya strip is the heritage exception. Population down 57% since 1992 leaves older stock and thinning footfall; subarea claims marked partial.
- **Q03 — answered**: Transport 8 = three in-polygon BTS stations + SRT Wongwian Yai terminal + major bus hub + 12 main roads/4 bridges (BTS Wongwian Yai itself across the boundary). Rent 9 = cheapest portal pricing of the batch (1-bed avg USD 387, page-one from 11,500 THB) on falling demand. Food 8 = Talat Phlu's decades-old shop layer plus neighbourhood markets. - **Q04 — answered**: Green 4 is confirmed as honestly low - no district-scale park exists in the khet (two OSM passes + both wikis agree; the main named green is a ~10-rai pocket park). No hospital is verified inside the khet (Siriraj is Bangkok Noi; Somdech Phra Pinklao excluded by polygon). The brief's 'between the river and the expressway' framing does not hold - the expressway runs through neighbouring khets.
- **Q05 — answered**: Market mornings at Talat Phlu; school and commute pulses along the BTS band and Wongwian Yai junction; the Maeklong train passes through the market street on schedule; evenings are local and early - the khet has no late-night anchor of its own.
- **Q06 — answered**: Flat canal-grid streets; the Talat Phlu market-to-BTS relationship (~1 km walk per TAT) and the rail-adjacent shop lines make the central khwaeng walkable at block scale; Intharaphitak/Isaraphap traffic is the hostile axis; no walking durations were derived from catchment radius in this bundle.
- **Q07 — answered**: Everyday needs: Talat Phlu, Samre and Wat Sutharam markets (OSM-mapped) plus the BTS-band malls; schools incl. - **Q08 — answered**: App 12,000/24,000 matches HipFlat's portal data closely (studio USD 355 ~ 12-13k, 1-bed USD 387 ~ 13-14k, 2-bed USD 652 ~ 22-23k; Talat Phlu cluster 14,500-21,000) - the cheapest of the four batch khets, with prices still falling (-7.0% vs Jan 2026). Rent 9 is defensible.
- **Q09 — answered**: Green: app 4.6 ha (0.6%) - the batch's lowest - is consistent with the map (pocket park ~10 rai + sports fields; no district park). Quiet: the interior khwaeng are genuinely quiet in a shrinking-population way; the riverside walkway (05:00-22:00) is the main new public space, and it is a walkway, not a park.
- **Q10 — answered**: Strong fit: families and long-stayers who want the cheapest rents in the researched core with BTS access, old-market food and heritage riverfronts on weekends. Weak fit: park users (0.6% share), late-night dwellers, and anyone needing a full-service hospital inside the khet.
- **Q11 — answered**: MRT Purple Line southern extension construction through the khet (Wongwian Yai underground interchange; ~2030 target reported); Talat Phlu's 2024 film-fame bump to its food-street profile; the 2021-2023 riverside walkway from the Memorial Bridge to Wat Kalayanamit now in daily public use; portal rents declining through 2026.
- **Q12 — answered**: Unresolved: the app's 4.6-ha park composition (pocket-park aggregation presumed); Somdech Phra Pinklao Hospital's khet membership (wikis list it, polygon excludes it); Purple Line opening date (reported ~2030, snippet-level); the Maeklong line's service frequency; the Kudi Chin walkway's formal opening date (walkable since 2023 per aggregated reports). ## Claim index

- `thon-buri-c01` **[corroborated·neutral·entire_unit]** Thon Buri is one of Bangkok's 50 khets, 8.551 km2 on the west bank, with seven khwaeng: Wat Kanlaya (0.785 km2, 7,739 people), Hiran Ruchi (0.691, 10,788), Bang Yi Ruea (1.523, 18,470), Bukkhalo (1.210, 16,075), Talat Phlu (1.823, 15,221), Dao Khanong (1.289, 16,581) and Samre (1.230, 13,412) - 98,286 registered residents in Dec 2023. Boundaries (Thai Wikipedia): north - Bangkok Yai and Phra Nakhon along Khlong Bangkok Yai and the river midline; east - Khlong San via the Memorial Bridge, Prajadhipok, Lat Ya and Somdet Chao Phraya roads and Khlong Bang Sai Kai; south - Bang Kho Laem and Rat Burana along the river and Khlong Dao Khanong; west - Chom Thong and Phasi Charoen along Khlong Bang Sakae and allies. History: the area was King Taksin's capital 1767-1782; the amphoe was Ratchakhrue, renamed Bang Yi Ruea (11 Jul 1916) then Thon Buri (17 Apr 1939), becoming a khet in 1972; khwaeng Samre and Dao Khanong were created 1 Dec 2003. _(scope: district — Khet Thon Buri)_
- `thon-buri-c02` **[proposed·neutral·outside]** Wongwian Yai BTS terminus is NOT in the khet: English Wikipedia's station infobox places it in Khlong San District (opened 15 May 2009 under the name Yaek Taksin, code S8), and both language wikis list it under Khlong San's transport; the app's thon-buri polygon excludes the station point while including the Wongwian Yai circle and its King Taksin monument (English Wikipedia notes the monument itself is 'technically on the Khlong San side'). Thon Buri's own BTS Silom stations are Pho Nimit, Talat Phlu and Wutthakat - all three PIP-verified inside the app polygon. The SRT Wongwian Yai terminal of the Maeklong line IS in the khet, in khwaeng Bang Yi Ruea per English Wikipedia, and is PIP-IN. _(scope: venue — BTS Wongwian Yai / SRT Wongwian Yai)_
- `thon-buri-c03` **[corroborated·strength·entire_unit]** Rail heritage doubles as transit: the Maeklong-line railway terminates at SRT Wongwian Yai (in khwaeng Bang Yi Ruea) with a second stop at Talat Phlu station 'located right next to the market' (TAT), giving the khet a slow commuter line the BTS corridor parallels; the Wongwian Yai circle area is simultaneously the west bank's major bus hub (English Wikipedia). _(scope: corridor — Wongwian Yai - Talat Phlu rail corridor)_
- `thon-buri-c04` **[corroborated·neutral·entire_unit]** Registered population has collapsed from 231,271 (1992) to 98,286 (Dec 2023) - a 57% fall over three decades (Thai Wikipedia) - the steepest decline among the four khets in this batch; English Wikipedia's 2017 figure (109,482) brackets the curve. The app's 103,377 sits between the two wiki datapoints. Mechanically this is old low-rise stock losing households to inheritance, condominium conversion and family out-migration - the background to rent 9 and the old-community vibe. _(scope: district — Khet Thon Buri)_
- `thon-buri-c05` **[proposed·strength·entire_unit]** Rental market (HipFlat, October 2026): 900 listings, average asking USD 355 for studios, USD 387 for 1-bedrooms and USD 652 for 2-bedrooms (USD 13/sqm), page-one units from THB 11,500; clusters sit on the BTS band - Talat Phlu (multiple Life Sathorn Sierra units at 14,500-21,000 THB), Pho Nimit (Ideo Sathorn-Thapra, Parkland Taksin-Thapra from 12,000), Wutthakat (Metro Sky 13,000-16,000) and Wongwian Yai (Motif, with a free shuttle to the BTS).
