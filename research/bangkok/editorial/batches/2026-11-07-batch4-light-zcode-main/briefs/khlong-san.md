## Card preview (proposed)
> Old west-bank canalside sois on one side, ICONSIAM's billion-dollar riverfront on the other - Khlong San is the rare khet where a mega-mall is your neighbourhood amenity and your nearest public park is a memorial garden.

## Guide lead (proposed)
Khlong San wraps four khwaeng of west-bank sois, temples and piers around the Charoen Nakhon riverfront, where ICONSIAM (opened 2018, revenues up to 5.4bn THB in 2024) sets the register for everything from rents to dining. Transit is unusually deep for the west bank - the Gold Line's Khlong San terminus at Taksin Hospital, BTS Silom stops, express boats and 11+ mapped piers - while public green is thin (5.3 ha, 0.9%). The 2021 loss of the 40-year Khlong San Plaza market to an SRT rent dispute marked the turn from market-khet to tower-khet; registered population has fallen to 64,961.

## Strengths
- Transit depth without a rail-hub address: a Gold Line terminus at Taksin Hospital, BTS Silom stops at both ends of the khet, two bridges and a dense pier layer for river commuting. *[`khlong-san-c02`]*
- The riverfront-amenity trade: mall-grade dining, retail and river decks at ICONSIAM/ICS alongside heritage anchors (Lhong 1919, Princess Mother Memorial Park) that cost nothing to visit. *[`khlong-san-c03`, `khlong-san-c06`]*

## Trade-offs
- Green 7 is buffer-dependent: the khet holds just 5.3 ha of park (0.9% share), the riverside 'park' is private mall ground, and the named public parks sit at polygon edges. *[`khlong-san-c07`]*
- The everyday-market layer lost its 40-year anchor when Khlong San Plaza closed in 2021 over an SRT rent jump (1.6M -> 15M THB), and no replacement development is confirmed. *[`khlong-san-c04`]*

## Claim index

- `khlong-san-c01` **[corroborated·neutral·entire_unit]** Khlong San is one of Bangkok's oldest khets, 6.051 km2 on the west bank's easternmost edge, with four khwaeng: Somdet Chao Phraya (1.317 km2, 11,444 people), Khlong San (0.727, 13,160), Bang Lamphu Lang (2.234, 22,471) and Khlong Ton Sai (1.773, 17,886) - 64,961 registered residents in Dec 2023 (Thai Wikipedia), 73,263 in 2017 (English Wikipedia). Boundaries: the Chao Phraya river on the north/east/south (across Phra Nakhon, Samphanthawong, Bang Rak, Sathon, Bang Kho Laem); the only land neighbour is Thon Buri, separated by Khlong Bang Sai Kai, Somdet Chao Phraya (Taksin) Road and Prajadhipok Road. History: originally Amphoe Bang Lamphu Lang, renamed Buppharam, then Khlong San (1916); named for the Khlong San/Prasan canal that once ran through longkong orchards, filled in roughly 70-80 years ago for Lat Ya, Prajadhipok and Khlong San roads; the Maeklong Railway's original terminus was here until the line was cut back to Wongwian Yai in 1961. _(scope: district — Khet Khlong San)_
- `khlong-san-c02` **[corroborated·strength·entire_unit]** Transit serving the khet (both wikis' lists): BTS Silom Line's Krung Thon Buri and Wongwian Yai stations, and the BTS Gold Line's three stops (Krung Thon Buri, Charoen Nakhon, Khlong San). Per the sibling st.khlong-san bundle, Gold Line G3 'Khlong San' (opened 16 Dec 2020, in front of Taksin Hospital) is the line's terminus and its OSM node sits inside the app district polygon; G2 Charoen Nakhon - the ICONSIAM stop - falls OUTSIDE the app district polygon, as does BTS Krung Thon Buri. Tha Din Daeng, Khlong San, Si Phraya (two gates), ICONSIAM 2/4 and other named ferry terminals are mapped along the khet's river frontage; both wikis list Tha Din Daeng and the Chao Phraya express-boat stops. _(scope: district — Khlong San riverfront + Gold Line)_
- `khlong-san-c03` **[conflicted·neutral·partial]** ICONSIAM is the district's economic anchor: opened 9 November 2018 on a 30-rai riverside plot with roughly 50 billion THB invested, revenues reported at ~2,000M THB (2020-21), 3,747M (2022) and 5,385M (2024), with 51 new brands committing ~1,500M THB around the 2025 anniversary and an 'ICONSIAM 2' project teased. The mall sits in khwaeng Wat Kanlaya, Khet Khlong San (batch-3 found it inside st.charoen-nakhon's catchment within this district), but the app's khlong-san district polygon EXCLUDES the ICONSIAM/ICS/River Park points (PIP-verified) - a polygon-vs-administration conflict the coordinator must resolve. _(scope: building — ICONSIAM, 299 Charoen Nakhon Rd)_
- `khlong-san-c04` **[proposed·tradeoff·entire_unit]** The 2021 Khlong San Plaza closure removed a 30-40-year-old wet-market/shopping staple: after the State Railway of Thailand raised the demanded rent from 1.6M to 15M THB, the operator declined renewal and began handing the ~5-rai site (adjacent to the cross-river ferry pier) back from 10 September 2021, with demolition to follow and SRT planning to re-auction the land; riverside land prices in the corridor had already risen to 400,000-500,000 THB/sq.wa, credited partly to the Gold Line. Nothing in body-read sources confirms a replacement anchor development on the plot as of 2026. _(scope: venue — Khlong San Plaza / SRT land by the Khlong San ferry pier)_
