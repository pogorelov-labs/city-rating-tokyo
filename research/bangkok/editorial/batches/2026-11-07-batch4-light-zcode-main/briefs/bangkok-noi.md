## Card preview (proposed)
> A hospital-and-railway district on the river: Siriraj anchors the bank, Wang Lang market feeds it daily, and the twice-daily Kanchanaburi local still steams out of Thonburi station - cheap Charan-corridor rents between.

## Guide lead (proposed)
Bangkok Noi is the west bank at its most working: Thailand's first western hospital, Siriraj, owns the riverfront, the old 1903 Bangkok Noi terminus survives as the Siriraj Bimuksthan museum, and the operating Thonburi station still sends two local trains a day toward Kanchanaburi - a 750-metre walk from the MRT. Wang Lang market runs daily at the pier; Central Pinklao anchors the retail. Two Blue Line stations (Fai Chai, Bang Khun Non) plus boat piers carry the commute. Rents track the Charan Sanitwong corridor (about 11k-23k in listed 2026 stock). The honest trade-offs: the green is riverside-and-temple rather than parkland, and convenience thins quickly inland.

## Strengths
- A rare working heritage-rail link: Thonburi station (khwaeng Siriraj) still runs the daily Kanchanaburi/Nam Tok locals, 750 m from MRT Bang Khun Non - verified in two independent rail sources. *[`bangkok-noi-c03`]*
- Food economy with a real anchor: Wang Lang market's daily 08:00-18:00 worker trade at the Siriraj pier, plus Pran Nok, Bang Khun Si and Chaitit markets and Central Pinklao for everything else. *[`bangkok-noi-c04`, `bangkok-noi-c06`]*

## Trade-offs
- Conveniences cluster on the river/MRT corner: largest khwaeng Bang Khun Si holds no MRT station in the data read - inland daily life leans on buses and SRT halts. *[`bangkok-noi-c08`]*
- Park share is 0.8% (9.4 ha); green means the riverbank, khlong rims and temple grounds rather than a destination park. *[`bangkok-noi-c10`]*

- **Q02 — answered**: Three daily lives: the Siriraj river corner (hospital workers, Wang Lang 8-18 daily), the Charan/Fai Chai condo corridor, and inland Bang Khun Si (bus/SRT-halt life). Inland texture thin in sources read.
- **Q03 — answered**: Transport 7: two MRT Blue stations + express-boat piers + functioning Thonburi terminus (two daily Kanchanaburi/Nam Tok locals; 750 m from Bang Khun Non MRT). Long-distance trains left for Krung Thep Aphiwat (19 Jan 2023), so the terminus is a commuter/heritage stop, not a regional gateway.
- **Q04 — answered**: Counter-view: amenity density collapses away from the river/MRT corner - largest khwaeng (Bang Khun Si) holds neither MRT station nor named mall in the data read; transport 7 (not higher) and DE 8 (mall+markets) both defensible but spatially uneven.
- **Q05 — answered**: Rhythm: hospital workdays feed Wang Lang 08:00-18:00; two daily Kanchanaburi locals (07:45 out per TrueID listing, snippet-flagged); express boats all day; temple calendar around Wat Suwannaram/Wat Rakhang unsourced.
- **Q06 — answered**: Walkability prose absent; sourced adjacency: Thonburi station 750 m from MRT Bang Khun Non (seat61 statement, not a radius conversion); Wang Lang starts at the pier. Riverside lanes and market sois imply fine-grained walking, unsourced.

## Claim index

- `bangkok-noi-c01` **[proposed·neutral·entire_unit]** Khet Bangkok Noi (บางกอกน้อย, geocode 1071) is a west-bank khet of 11.944 km2 with five khwaeng: Siri Rat 1.258 km2, Ban Chang Lo 2.076, Bang Khun Non 1.492, Bang Khun Si 4.360 (largest), Arun Amarin 2.758; English Wikipedia's table gives 112,046 residents at 2017 (app's 103,791 is a later vintage). Established as Amphoe Ammarin 15 Oct 1915, renamed Bangkok Noi 11 Jul 1916, became a khet in the 1972 merger, lost Bang Phlat on 9 Nov 1989, and gained its current khwaeng set when Arun Amarin was created on 12 Dec 1991. OSM relations: khet 2942282, khwaeng relations 11355120-24. _(scope: district — Bangkok Noi)_
- `bangkok-noi-c02` **[proposed·neutral·inside]** Siriraj Hospital - Thailand's first western hospital, with its medical/forensic museums - sits by the mouth of Khlong Bangkok Noi in khwaeng Siri Rat (verified, per the brief's tension item). Beside it stands the 1903 Bangkok Noi railway terminus (Karl Doehring's Brick Expressionism building, renamed Thon Buri in 1942, WWII Japanese base for Burma Railway supply, closed to trains 4 Oct 2003), reopened in 2013 as the Siriraj Bimuksthan Museum. _(scope: building — Siriraj Hospital / Siriraj Bimuksthan)_
- `bangkok-noi-c03` **[corroborated·neutral·inside]** The railway-village tension resolved: the operational terminus is Thon Buri station (สถานีธนบุรี) on Rotfai Road, khwaeng Siriraj - 'Terminus for ordinary and commuter trains', a class-1 station renamed from Bangkok Noi on 1 Jan 2004, next stop Charansanitwong Halt toward Taling Chan, and the Southern Line's kilometre 0 is still measured from it (EN Wikipedia). Seat 61 confirms current service: the two daily local trains to Kanchanaburi and Nam Tok depart from Thonburi, which is a stated 9-minute/750 m walk from MRT Bang Khun Non; long-distance services moved instead to Krung Thep Aphiwat on 19 Jan 2023. The SRT corridor still runs through the khet (Charansanitwong and Bang Ramat halts nearby). _(scope: venue — Thon Buri railway station)_
- `bangkok-noi-c04` **[corroborated·strength·inside]** Wang Lang Market (next to Siriraj, on Phra Nnok Road at the river) runs every day 08:00-18:00 with free entry; it is a worker-and-student food market famous for desserts and southern-Thai dishes, with almost no English signage, reached by the Chao Phraya express boat to Prannok (N10) or Wang Lang pier ferries to Tha Phra Chan and Tha Chang (Your Thai Guide; EN Wikipedia lists Wang Lang among khet markets with the piers). This is the concrete face of food 7. _(scope: venue — Wang Lang Market)_
- `bangkok-noi-c05` **[proposed·neutral·partial]** In-khet rail transit is two MRT Blue stations - Fai Chai and Bang Khun Non (EN Wikipedia; OSM nodes 7096345624 and 2094758837 both inside khet area 3602942281) - plus the river: Chao Phraya express boat piers N10 Prannok and N11 Thonburi Railway, with Wang Lang pier cross-river ferries. Station coverage concentrates on the khet's north-east river corner; the Charan Sanitwong corridor further southwest is served by the SRT halts only. _(scope: district — Bangkok Noi)_
- `bangkok-noi-c06` **[proposed·neutral·entire_unit]** Retail/DE anchor: CentralPlaza Pinklao on Borommaratchachonnani Road is the khet's main shopping center (EN Wikipedia), supplemented by named local markets - Wang Lang, Pran Nok, Bang Khun Si and Chaitit markets. Daily-essentials 8 is plausible for a district with a full-size regional mall plus four named markets and a major hospital economy. _(scope: district — Bangkok Noi)_
- `bangkok-noi-c07` **[proposed·neutral·partial]** Rent corroboration (rent 9, editorial 12,000/25,000): HipFlat shows 292 condo listings, portal averages USD 298 (studio) / 443 (1BR) / 654 (2BR), USD 14/sqm (Oct 2026; ~10.5k/15.5k/23k THB at ~35). Projects line the Charan Sanitwong / Fai Chai corridor (Ideo Mobi Charan Interchange - 1,196 units, completed 2015; The Tree Charan 30; Supalai Loft Yaek Fai Chai; SO Origin Siriraj). _(scope: corridor — Charan Sanitwong corridor (Bangkok Noi side))_
- `bangkok-noi-c08` **[proposed·mixed·partial]** Counter-view on evenness: the khet's conveniences hug the Siriraj/river corner and the Fai Chai-Bang Khun Non MRT pair; khwaeng Bang Khun Si (4.360 km2, the largest) has no MRT station inside it in the data read - its rail access is the SRT corridor (Charansanitwong/Bang Ramat halts) and bus roads. Transport 7 rather than 8-9 fits: strong on the river and north-east, thinner inland. _(scope: district — Bang Khun Si khwaeng)_
- `bangkok-noi-c09` **[corroborated·neutral·entire_unit]** Heritage and rhythm: the khet's older layer is canal-first - the district grew around Khlong Bangkok Noi (the Chao Phraya's former main channel), with the Royal Barges National Museum on the northern rim, Wat Suwannaram (a former royal cremation site that also hosted the old district office) and riverside Wat Rakhang on the other rim (EN Wikipedia). The weekly rhythm is set by the hospital economy (Siriraj staff and students feed Wang Lang daily) and the twice-daily Kanchanaburi local trains (seat 61; EN wiki services box) - a working district, not a leisure district. _(scope: district — Bangkok Noi)_
