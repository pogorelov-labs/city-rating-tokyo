## Card preview (proposed)
> The CBD's south annex: embassy rows, office towers and a renovated royal park along a canal being turned into Bangkok's first linear water-garden - at the batch's highest rents, with the BTS gates sitting on the boundary line the map excludes.

## Guide lead (proposed)
Sathon wraps three khwaeng of residential sois around the Sathorn office corridor, with the deepest rental stock of the batch (8,231 listings), 12+ embassies, Saint Louis Hospital, and the batch's best named-park access - the 80 Phansa royal park reopened in 2025-2026 with expanded green, and the 4.5 km Chong Nonsi canal park keeps extending even as its water quality lags. The honest asterisks: the BTS stations that give the district its name sit in the road median the app polygon excludes (transport 7, not 9), the 25-ha green figure needs a counting rule, and rents - though slipping - remain the priciest of the four researched khets.

## Strengths
- Parks you can actually use: the renovated 80 Phansa royal park (05:00-21:00, lit loop, playground, stage) plus the canal-park walkway segments and Taksin bridge park - the batch's strongest named-green access. *[`sathon-c05`, `sathon-c04`]*
- Office-district completeness: Saint Louis Hospital and schools, 1,098 food POIs, wet markets inland, and a BRT corridor threading Narathiwas - daily life rarely requires leaving the khet. *[`sathon-c08`, `sathon-c03`]*

## Trade-offs
- The transit paradox: three BTS stations carry the district's name but stand in the median the polygon excludes, leaving interior sois (Yen Akat, Chan, Nang Linchi) a kilometre-plus from any rail gate - hence transport 7. *[`sathon-c03`, `sathon-c12`]*
- Priciest of the batch: portal 1-bed averages ~USD 889 (~30k THB) with the blend at 25,000/55,000, and the canal park's water is still visibly poor despite the 2024-2026 build-out. *[`sathon-c07`, `sathon-c06`]*

## Claim index

- `sathon-c01` **[corroborated·neutral·entire_unit]** Sathon is one of Bangkok's 50 khets, 9.326 km2 south of the CBD, with three khwaeng: Thung Wat Don (3.195 km2, 34,618 people), Yan Nawa (2.090, 18,132) and Thung Maha Mek (4.041, 18,556) - 71,306 registered residents in Dec 2023. Boundaries (Thai Wikipedia): north - Bang Rak/Pathum Wan along Khlong Sathon and the south side of Rama IV; east - Khlong Toei/Yan Nawa along the Mae Nam railway line, Soi Chuea Phloeng 4 and Yen Akat road; south - Yan Nawa/Bang Kho Laem along Nang Linchi, Chan Kao, Khlong Chong Nonsi, Chan and Sathupradit roads to Khlong Khuay; west - the river midline (Khlong San across). The khet was created 9 November 1989 from Yan Nawa (branch office from 9 March 1989); the spelling สาธร was officially corrected in April 1999. _(scope: district — Khet Sathon)_
- `sathon-c02` **[corroborated·neutral·partial]** The office-district identity sits on a corridor the app polygon excludes: English Wikipedia states Sathon Road 'marks the northern boundary of the district', with the southern lanes in Sathon and the northern lanes across Sathon canal in Bang Rak - and PIP confirms the app polygon hugs the road's south side, putting the BTS Surasak, Chong Nonsi and Saint Louis station nodes (all standing in the road median) OUTSIDE the sathon polygon, along with office towers on the north side, while the south sidewalk is IN. The Empire Tower, AIA Sathorn Tower, At Sathorn Square, Robot Building and the 12+ embassies of Thai Wikipedia's list are the khet's daytime economy but mostly lie on or beyond the excluded strip; Saint Louis Hospital, MR Kukrit's House, Suan Phlu market and Wat Yannawa are PIP-IN. _(scope: corridor — Sathon Road corridor)_
- `sathon-c03` **[proposed·neutral·entire_unit]** Why transport 7 and not 9: the BTS Silom Line (Surasak, Chong Nonsi, Saint Louis, with Saphan Taksin at the north-west corner) and MRT Lumphini serve the khet's north and north-east edges only - and all three mid-corridor BTS nodes fall outside the app polygon, which begins at the south side of Sathorn Rd. The interior - Yen Akat, Chan, Nang Linchi, Thung Wat Don's sois - sits roughly a kilometre or more from any rail gate. The BRT's Chong Nonsi-Ratchaphruek corridor does cross the khet on Narathiwas Road (BRT Sathon stop PIP-IN), and the Chao Phraya express boat calls at Tha Sathorn; but the modal mix is edge-loaded, which is the honest mechanism for a 7. _(scope: district — Khet Sathon)_
- `sathon-c04` **[corroborated·tradeoff·entire_unit]** Green 9 against a 25.0 ha (3.5%) app figure does not reproduce from OSM: named in-polygon parks computed at 80 Phansa royal park 3.02 ha, Taksin bridge park 0.89 ha, Khlong Chong Nonsi canal park 0.36 ha mapped so far, the Yudhi under-expressway park 1.02 ha, plus small plots - roughly 5-6 ha PIP-IN, with the 2.63-ha Suan Pa Chaloem Phra Kiat at the far-west tip PIP-OUT.
