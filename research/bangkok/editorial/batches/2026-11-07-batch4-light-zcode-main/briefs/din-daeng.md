## Card preview (proposed)
> A two-speed khet: market-sois and housing blocks in khwaeng Din Daeng, and a four-station MRT strip with night markets on Ratchadaphisek - all under the city's loudest measured skyline of traffic.

## Guide lead (proposed)
Din Daeng packs city-hall offices, a top-tier daily-essentials layer and four MRT Blue Line stations into 8.35 km2 between Vibhavadi and Ratchadaphisek, with rents from the mid-teens - but it is also the district Bangkok's own livability index put last in 2025, and the Pollution Control Department's noisiest monitoring point (71.6-81.6 dB(A), every measured day over standard). Living here means corridor convenience with corridor noise, markets at the door, and a forest strip rather than a park.

## Strengths
- Four in-polygon MRT Blue stations (Phra Ram 9, Cultural Centre, Huai Khwang, Sutthisan) give the khet a genuine rail spine without a BTS wait-list price. *[`din-daeng-c02`]*
- Daily life is dense and cheap: wet markets plus Big C Extra and Fortune Town on the corridor, 1-bed rents of roughly 13-27k THB, and the Jodd Fairs night-market strip running 17:00-01:00. *[`din-daeng-c08`, `din-daeng-c05`, `din-daeng-c06`]*

## Trade-offs
- Objective environmental strain: the PCD's Din Daeng station is the city's noisiest (71.6-81.6 dB(A), 100% of measured days over the 70 dB(A) standard), and Rocket Media Lab's Bangkok Index 2025 ranks the khet last of 50 (17.35/50). *[`din-daeng-c03`, `din-daeng-c04`]*
- Green 8 overstates what the map shows: OSM named public green inside the khet sums to roughly 3 ha, led by the 1.53-ha Vibhavadi forest strip - the 15.5 ha app figure needs a methodology check. *[`din-daeng-c10`]*

## Question coverage
- **Q01 — answered**: Khet Din Daeng, 8.354 km2, two khwaeng (Din Daeng 4.618 km2/68,251; Ratchadaphisek 3.736 km2/41,551 created 2017), bounded by Khlong Bang Sue (N), Ratchadaphisek + Asoke-Din Daeng roads (E), Khlong Sam Sen (S), Vibhavadi Rangsit Rd (W); khet effective 14 Jan 1994. App-polygon landmarks IN: City Hall 2, UTCC, SET, Fortune Town, Jodd Fairs Ratchada, Thailand Cultural Centre, Vibhavadi forest park, MRT Blue Phra Ram 9/Cultural Centre/Huai Khwang/Sutthisan. OUT: MRT Phetchaburi, ARL Makkasan, Esplanade, The Street Ratchada, Army Sports Stadium, Saphan Khwai BTS. Khwaeng-subarea claims marked partial.
- **Q02 — answered**: Two different halves: khwaeng Din Daeng is the flats-and-markets side (National Housing Authority flats, Din Daeng/Kwan Phatthana wet markets, City Hall 2, Vibhavadi forest strip), while khwaeng Ratchadaphisek is the transit-and-entertainment corridor (four MRT stations, Fortune Town, Jodd Fairs, Big C Extra, the Cultural Centre). Everyday life for residents runs through wet markets and sois; the corridor provides transit and evening economy. Partial by khwaeng.
- **Q03 — answered**: Transport 8 = four in-polygon MRT Blue stations on the eastern corridor (Phetchaburi/Makkasan cluster at the tip is outside the app polygon; no BTS inside; red-line missing link not built, ~2028 start reported). DE 9 = market-plus-mall density along one corridor. Rent 8 = mid-market portal pricing (1-bed 13-27k). Food 8 / nightlife 8 = 1,322 food + 85 nightlife POIs concentrating on Ratchada. Crowd 2 = Vibhavadi/Ratchadaphisek/expressway through-traffic plus top-tier density - and the noise record proves it.
- **Q04 — answered**: Three: (1) Bangkok Index 2025 ranks Din Daeng last of 50 khets (17.35/50, down from 32nd) - the strongest published counter-signal to the app's positive scorecard; (2) the PCD noise record (71.6-81.6 dB(A), 182/182 days over standard) gives safety 5 its only objective physical correlate - environmental strain rather than crime; (3) green 8 cannot be reproduced from OSM named green (~3 ha vs app 15.5 ha).
- **Q05 — answered**: Morning-evening commute pulses along Ratchadaphisek's four MRT stations and Vibhavadi's arterial flow; the evening economy (Jodd Fairs 17:00-01:00) concentrates on the corridor while the flat sois empty early; noise peaks with traffic - the monitoring data shows the standard is exceeded every single measured day, i.e. there is no quiet hour at the measurement point.
- **Q06 — answered**: Flat khet on a rectilinear grid; the market layer (Din Daeng, SRI Din Daeng, Kwan Phatthana markets) and the MRT stations line the two corridor roads, so everyday walking is block-scale along Din Daeng and Ratchadaphisek; crossing Vibhavadi and the expressway ramps is the hostile part. No walking durations were derived from catchment radius in this bundle.
- **Q07 — answered**: Daily needs: wet markets + Big C Extra + Fortune Town on the corridor; sports: Thai-Japanese Stadium, Wesn 2, Kheha fields, commercial gyms; transit: four MRT stations; evenings: Jodd Fairs strip. What the khet lacks is large green and quiet - the forest strip (08:00-18:00 dog-friendly) is the only substantial park.
- **Q08 — answered**: App 13,000/27,000 is consistent with HipFlat's portal data (studio avg USD385 ~13-14k THB, 1-bed USD446 ~15-16k, 2-bed USD710 ~25k; page-one 1-beds 13,090-27,000) given the khet's stock of older flats and mid-market condos; the trend is slightly negative (-6.3% vs Jan 2026). Rent 8 reads as 'cheap for the CBD-adjacent core', which the numbers support.
- **Q09 — answered**: Green honesty: app 15.5 ha (1.8%) vs ~3 ha OSM named public green (Vibhavadi forest park 1.53 ha is the largest) - green 8 is generous unless the app counts road-planting/stadium grounds; coordinator methodology call needed. Quiet honesty: objectively the city's noisiest monitoring point (71.6-81.6 dB(A), 100% of days) - quiet is only found in interior soi blocks away from the corridors.
- **Q10 — answered**: Strong fit: commuters who live on the Ratchada corridor and want cheap-for-location rents with markets at the door and late-night food. Weak fit: anyone ranking quiet, park access, or environmental comfort first - the livability index and the noise record both point the other way; also weak for people who need BTS (there is none) or river access.
- **Q11 — answered**: Jodd Fairs Rama 9 closed Jan 2025 and the consolidated Jodd Fairs Ratchada opened 1 Nov 2024 inside the khet; Bangkok Index livability fell to last place (2025); the 28 Jun 2026 BMC seat flipped to the People's Party; portal rents drifting down; SRT red-line missing link through the khet remains at planning/construction-start ~2028.
- **Q12 — answered**: Unresolved: the park_ha 15.5 counting rule (OSM named green ~3 ha); the identity of the app's food/nightlife POI mix; safety 5 has no statistics (perception label recommended); TNN's 17.35 figure is snippet-corroborated only (article 403); the two planned-rail nodes (Orange/Grey 'Din Daeng', 'Pracha Songkhro') are construction-stage projects, not services; 'Soi Intharam 49' is Khaosod's transliteration and was not geolocated. Anchors verified against OSM with no mismatch.

## Claim index

- `din-daeng-c01` **[corroborated·neutral·entire_unit]** Din Daeng is one of Bangkok's 50 khets, 8.354 km2, split into only two khwaeng: Din Daeng (4.618 km2, 68,251 people Dec 2023) and Ratchadaphisek (3.736 km2, 41,551; carved out of khwaeng Din Daeng on 26 July 2017 along Mit Maitri and Pracha Songkhro roads). Boundaries (Thai Wikipedia): north - Chatuchak along Khlong Bang Sue, Khlong Phraya Woek and Khlong Nam Kheaw; east - Huai Khwang along Ratchadaphisek and Asoke-Din Daeng roads; south - Ratchathewi along Khlong Sam Sen; west - Phaya Thai along Vibhavadi Rangsit Road. The khet was announced 8 Oct 1993 and took effect 14 January 1994, split from Phaya Thai; the name comes from the red laterite-dust road built in the 1940s under Field Marshal P. Pibulsongkram. Landmarks inside the app polygon (PIP-verified): BMA City Hall 2, University of the Thai Chamber of Commerce, the Stock Exchange of Thailand, Fortune Town, Jodd Fairs Ratchada, the Thailand Cultural Centre, Vibhavadi Rangsit Forest Park, and MRT Blue Line stations Phra Ram 9, Thailand Cultural Centre, Huai Khwang and Sutthisan. Outside the app polygon: MRT Phetchaburi and ARL Makkasan at the southern tip, the Esplanade and The Street Ratchada (across Ratchadaphisek in Huai Khwang), Saphan Khwai BTS, and the Army Sports Stadium. _(scope: district — Khet Din Daeng)_
- `din-daeng-c02` **[corroborated·neutral·entire_unit]** Din Daeng's transport 8 rests on four MRT Blue Line stations strung along its eastern Ratchadaphisek corridor (Phra Ram 9, Thailand Cultural Centre, Huai Khwang, Sutthisan) - all four fall inside the app polygon - with the ARL/Makkasan + MRT Phetchaburi cluster at the south-western tip just outside the polygon (both PIP-verified OUT; the khwaeng Makkasan holding them belongs to Ratchathewi per batch-3). There is no BTS or Airport Rail Link station inside the khet. The SRT dark-red-line 'missing link' (Krung Thep Aphiwat-Hua Mak) would pass through the Din Daeng/Ari station area but is not built: reporting located in Thai search results puts its construction start around 2028. Thai Wikipedia additionally lists planned Orange Line (Din Daeng, Pracha Songkhro, Cultural Centre) and Grey Line (Pracha Songkhro, Mit Maitri, Din Daeng) stations. _(scope: corridor — Ratchadaphisek MRT corridor)_
- `din-daeng-c03` **[corroborated·tradeoff·entire_unit]** Din Daeng is measurably the noisiest district in Bangkok: the Pollution Control Department's Khet-community Din Daeng monitoring station by Din Daeng Road averaged 71.6-81.6 dB(A) over 24 hours and exceeded the 70 dB(A) standard on 182 of 182 measured days (100%), ranking first citywide in the 2019 reporting window; the PCD names motorcycles, cars and cars with modified exhausts as the dominant urban noise source. English Wikipedia's environmental section repeats the 71.6-81.6 dB figure as the city's highest district average. _(scope: district — Khet-community Din Daeng noise station, Din Daeng Road)_
- `din-daeng-c04` **[corroborated·tradeoff·entire_unit]** In Rocket Media Lab's Bangkok Index 2025, Din Daeng ranked last of Bangkok's 50 districts for livability with 17.35 of 50 points, falling 18 places from 32nd in the 2024 edition - the largest decline reported; English Wikipedia's politics/environment coverage cites the same last-place 2026 result. Counter-evidence against the app's otherwise positive scorecard (transport 8, DE 9, vibe 8). _(scope: district — Khet Din Daeng)_
- `din-daeng-c05` **[proposed·neutral·entire_unit]** Rental market (HipFlat, October 2026): 519 condo listings in the Din Daeng search, average asking USD 385 for studios, USD 446 for 1-bedrooms and USD 710 for 2-bedrooms (USD 14/sqm overall), with page-one 1-bedrooms from THB 13,090 (The Maple) to THB 27,000 (Aspire Vibha Victory) and a studio from THB 12,000; prices per sqm are drifting down (-6.3% vs January 2026).
