## Card preview (proposed)
> Bangkok's self-declared new CBD: a Blue Line junction stitched into a mall and office cluster, with the city's densest Chinese-food corridors a stop away — still more construction site than finished district.

## Guide lead (proposed)
Phra Ram 9 pairs a 2004 MRT stop with the Ratchada-Rama 9 office corridor — G Tower and its neighbours already standing, Central Rama 9 rebranding as 'Central GR9' with retail doubling from early 2028 — and a catchment that is majority Din Daeng, cheaper and quieter than its CBD label suggests.

## Strengths
- Genuine CBD mechanics: an underground walkway into Central Rama 9, Fortune Town across the junction, Grade A offices open and a two-line future once the Orange Line arrives. *[`phra-ram-9-c02`, `phra-ram-9-c03`, `phra-ram-9-c09`]*
- Value: listing evidence puts 1-beds around 15-21k against app's 14,000 editorial blend, with the corridor's Chinese-community infrastructure making daily life cheap and complete. *[`phra-ram-9-c07`, `phra-ram-9-c06`]*

## Trade-offs
- The nightlife anchor is gone: Jodd Fairs' Rama 9 site closed at lease end and was demolished by January 2025, moving the late-night draw to Ratchada near Thailand Cultural Centre. *[`phra-ram-9-c05`]*
- Green is pocket-scale (0.7 ha, unnamed) and the CBD promise is partly prospective — the mall's retail redo does not reopen before early 2028. *[`phra-ram-9-c08`, `phra-ram-9-c04`]*
