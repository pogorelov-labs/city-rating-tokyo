## Card preview (proposed)
> One stop south of Siam, Ratchadamri trades mall crowds for the city's most private address strip: a station with a skyway into the St Regis, the 36.3 ha members-only racecourse across the road, and Lumphini Park's west gate at the bottom of the block - with One Bangkok's $3.9 billion district now open at its southern edge.

## Guide lead (proposed)
Ratchadamri is the quiet half of Pathum Wan's headline scores: the same 9-10s for transport, gym and green as its neighbors, but with a fifth of their food stock - because this is club land, hotel land and condo land, not mall land. Its two green masses tell the unit's real story: a racecourse you can enter a dozen Sundays a year, and a park that starts a few hundred meters from the platform.

## Strengths
- Green adjacency with genuine scale: Lumphini Park's west edge (57.6 ha total, 04:30-22:00) opens a few hundred meters from the station, and the RBSC's 36.3 ha of turf define the streetscape even when its gates are closed. *[`ratchadamri-c04`, `ratchadamri-c03`]*
- Quiet luxury infrastructure: S1 station with a concourse skyway to the St Regis, the 354-room Anantara Siam at street level, and One Bangkok's phased opening pushing the district's luxury center to the unit's edge. *[`ratchadamri-c01`, `ratchadamri-c02`, `ratchadamri-c06`]*

## Trade-offs
- The green=10 headline overstates usable green: the RBSC admits the public only on alternate-Sunday race days, and Lumphini's verified in-unit share is a clipped fraction - the park you can actually use is smaller than the score implies. *[`ratchadamri-c05`, `ratchadamri-c04`]*
- Food and nightlife run at a quarter to a fifth of Siam's density (160/25/17 vs 676/98/36): everyday eating means hotel prices or a walk to the seam with Ratchaprasong. *[`ratchadamri-c07`]*
- The 38,000/85,000 rent blend understates the strip: district 2-bed asks average 99,600 and specific Ratchadamri buildings list far higher - the number is a district average, not this street's market. *[`ratchadamri-c08`]*

## Day / night
- Dawn starts at Lumphini's 04:30 gate with the jogging crowd; weekdays hum quietly on hotel and office schedules; alternate Sundays bring race-day crowds to the Henri Dunant corner; after dark the unit reverts to residential-hotel quiet, one skywalk away from Ratchaprasong's lights. *[`ratchadamri-c11`, `ratchadamri-c03`, `ratchadamri-c04`]*

## Within-area variation
- Three bands: the Ratchaprasong seam at the north (skywalks, mall spillover, CentralWorld's edge), the hotel-club middle (St Regis, Anantara, RBSC fencing), and the Rama IV approach at the south where One Bangkok's hoardings and new plazas are actively rewriting the streetscape. *[`ratchadamri-c01`, `ratchadamri-c03`, `ratchadamri-c06`]*

## Conditional fit
- Fit for quiet-luxury renters, runners and race-day regulars who want park access and hotel services without Siam's crowds - and who are comfortable with the district-blend rent number understating their actual building. *[`ratchadamri-c11`, `ratchadamri-c04`, `ratchadamri-c08`]*
- Poor fit for budget diners and nightlife-first residents: the in-unit food layer is thin, the best green is partly private, and the quiet is the product being sold. *[`ratchadamri-c07`, `ratchadamri-c05`]*

- `ratchadamri-c02` **[proposed·neutral·inside]** The hotel strip is the unit's identity: Anantara Siam Bangkok Hotel at 155 Ratchadamri Road opposite the Royal Bangkok Sports Club (354 rooms, 8 restaurants) opened in 1978 as a Peninsula-site property, became Regent Bangkok, was named Four Seasons Bangkok in 2003 and rebranded Anantara Siam on 1 March 2015 under Minor Hotels - the station page lists it at street level beside the St Regis skyway. _(scope: venue — Anantara Siam Bangkok Hotel, 155 Ratchadamri Rd)_
- `ratchadamri-c03` **[proposed·neutral·partial]** The Royal Bangkok Sports Club - founded 1901, 227 rai (36.3 ha, 90 acres) between Henri Dunant Road and Ratchadamri Road, membership capped at 12,500 with waiting lists up to a decade - borders the unit on its northwest side. - `ratchadamri-c04` **[corroborated·strength·partial]** Lumphini Park (57.6 ha, created 1925 from King Rama VI's donated land, bounded by Ratchadamri Road on its west, Rama IV on its south, Witthayu on its east; OSM opening hours 04:30-22:00) is the unit's public green anchor: the park's western edge lies a few hundred meters from the station even though the polygon centroid sits 1,028 m away.
