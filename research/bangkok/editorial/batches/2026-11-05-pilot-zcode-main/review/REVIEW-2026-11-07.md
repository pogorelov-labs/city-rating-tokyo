   text, radius→walk-time patterns, claim-ID prefixes, QA↔claim reference
   closure, and honesty of `review.checks`.
2. **Independent geometry re-computation**: fetched authoritative coordinates
   (Wikipedia infoboxes) for 12 boundary-critical places and ran haversine
   distances from the app's rated-stop centers plus point-in-polygon tests
   against the app's own `station-geometry.json` polygons (ray-casting,
   corrected after a first-pass formula bug — initial PIP run produced two
   false "inside" results and was rerun).
3. **Source re-reads** for the most consequential claims: 19 re-reads across
   15 sources (details §4).
