## Card preview (proposed)

## Guide lead (proposed)
Asok is Bangkok's crossroads more than its neighborhood: the only place where the BTS Sukhumvit Line and the MRT Blue Line meet, with Terminal 21 at the corner, Exchange Tower offices on one side and the Soi Cowboy bar strip about a block away. Guides rate it the easiest place in Bangkok to live without a car, and the 800 m catchment is dense with convenience stores, supermarkets and cheap soi food. The price of that position is paid daily — one of the city's most crowded station platforms, noise from two roads, two rail lines and the bar soi, and footpaths old enough that the city government announced their rebuild in 2025. It fits people who will use the interchange every day; it wears down people who will hear it from home.

## Strengths
- The two-line interchange means most of central Bangkok is reachable without a car, and guides describe residents here giving up taxis almost entirely. *[`asok-c11`, `asok-c01`]*
- Everyday essentials are unusually dense within 800 m: 61 convenience stores, eight supermarket-type stores (Tops, Foodland, Villa Market, Makro Food Service among them) and two marketplaces. *[`asok-c16`]*
- Eating out runs from a Korean community cluster on Soi 12 to Middle Eastern and Thai soi kitchens and Terminal 21's food court, so daily food does not require traveling. *[`asok-c13`, `asok-c03`]*
- The northern tip of Benjakitti Park crosses into the catchment, putting a running-loop edge within a few hundred meters even though the main entrances sit farther out. *[`asok-c04`, `asok-c18`]*

## Trade-offs
- Crowding is structural, not occasional: a 2016 editorial ranked Asok's crowds 1/5, and when one BTS train failed at the evening rush in September 2026, passengers backed up from Asok's platform to the street-level gates. *[`asok-c08`, `asok-c09`]*
- Noise stacks up from three directions — Sukhumvit and Asok Montri roads, the two rail viaducts, and the Soi Cowboy strip — which is why guides tell renters to go five minutes deep into a side soi. *[`asok-c14`, `asok-c02`]*
- The walk to the stations ran for decades on 30-year-old paving tiles that collapse and splash mud; the BMA promised a rebuild starting 2025 but completion is not yet verified. *[`asok-c10`]*
- Rents carry a location premium and the nearest full hospital (Bumrungrad) is about 1.2 km out — everyday medicine here means pharmacies and small clinics. *[`asok-c12`, `asok-c17`]*

## Day / night
- Weekday mornings and early evenings belong to office commuters squeezing through the interchange, while late evening shifts the center of gravity to the ~40 bars of Soi Cowboy and the tourist flow around Terminal 21 — the same corner, two different crowds. *[`asok-c07`, `asok-c02`, `asok-c08`]*
- Green time is bounded by park hours (04:30 to about 21:00-22:00), so the quietest window in the unit is early morning before the office rush fills Asok Montri. *[`asok-c18`, `asok-c14`]*

## Within-area variation
- The difference between blocks is bigger than the difference between hours: Cowboy's bar row sits ~120 m from the interchange while the mouths of Sois 15, 19 and 23 carry office and apartment life, and Soi 11 mixes guesthouses with residences a few hundred meters west. *[`asok-c02`, `asok-c06`, `asok-c13`]*
- South of Sukhumvit, the catchment crosses into Khlong Toei district and reaches the edge of Benjakitti's lake-and-forest section; park-facing addresses there are explicitly marketed at a premium over sois without a view. *[`asok-c04`, `asok-c18`]*
- Guides characterize the area as having less identity than Thonglor or Silom — it is picked for the crossroads, which is why side-soi selection (window orientation, distance from the main road) does most of the work in whether a given unit is livable. *[`asok-c15`, `asok-c14`]*

## Conditional fit
- Strong fit: commuters who change lines daily, airport travelers via Makkasan, and workers in the surrounding towers who want a car-free routine. *[`asok-c11`, `asok-c19`, `asok-c07`]*
- Poor fit: quiet-seekers and home-all-day remote workers, per the guides' own verdicts; families wanting calm are pointed one stop east to Phrom Phong. *[`asok-c19`, `asok-c14`]*
- Situational fit: nightlife workers and frequent visitors trade the Cowboy strip's convenience against its noise; anyone prioritizing park life should treat Benjakitti as a park whose nearest edge is about 460 m away and whose main entrances lie beyond the catchment, not a doorstep one. *[`asok-c02`, `asok-c04`, `asok-c18`]*
