## Card preview (proposed)
> A 314-metre tower at the station door, BRT stairs off the same skywalk, and a single 200-metre canal park section that took four more years of stalled phases to stand alone — Chong Nonsi is an office interchange with a residential fringe, not a finished neighborhood.

## Guide lead (proposed)
Chong Nonsi is where the BTS Silom line crosses Narathiwas Road at the Sathorn junction, ringed by MahaNakhon, Empire Tower and Sathorn Square within a few hundred metres. The unit's everyday logic is an office clock: pavement food stalls and the weekday-only Lalai Sap market at lunch, an elevated walkway system for the heat and rain, then a widely described emptying after 6 pm and on Sundays. Its one new public space, the Chong Nonsi Canal Park, opened as a 200 m section by the station in December 2021 and the rest of the 4.5 km plan has stalled. Sala Daeng and the edge of the Patpong streets sit about 660–740 m north, so the catchment's nightlife is a fringe phenomenon, not the unit's own street life.

## Strengths
- Transit is stacked rather than merely present: BTS on the Silom line, the BRT terminus off the same skywalk (modernized with electric buses and a flat 15 THB fare in 2024), and a river-pier option one stop away. *[`chong-nonsi-c01`, `chong-nonsi-c09`]*
- Landmark employers and addresses are at walking distance as geometry, not marketing: MahaNakhon roughly 110–120 m and Empire Tower roughly 400 m from the platform. *[`chong-nonsi-c02`]*
- Weekday food is dense and cheap by design — pavement stalls feeding an office lunch crowd, plus the weekday-morning Lalai Sap market on Silom Soi 5 near the unit's northern edge. *[`chong-nonsi-c10`]*
- The canal park section is a real, stair-accessible new public space with a plaza, water features and a universal-design ramp, reachable without crossing a signal-free main road at the skywalk point. *[`chong-nonsi-c06`]*

## Trade-offs
- The weekend empties out: five independent texts describe Sunday inactivity and ghost-town blocks, and two of them call the effect unsettling rather than merely quiet. *[`chong-nonsi-c14`]*
- Rent data at the unit level is thin and skewed: a 24,000 THB one-bed median rests on 7 listings, and the 72,000 THB two-bed median is inflated by 72–93 sq m serviced and luxury stock above the reference band. *[`chong-nonsi-c12`, `chong-nonsi-c13`]*
- The canal park debate was never resolved in the sources: critics called it greenwashing on a traffic-choked canal with only half the entrances signalized and no wheelchair access at the BRT point, and no later source confirms fixes. *[`chong-nonsi-c07`]*
- Corridor traffic is a daily tax: peak-hour Sathorn Road taxis are described as unusable and Narathiwas carries a claimed ~300,000 vehicles a day, with road-facing buildings catching the noise. *[`chong-nonsi-c15`]*

## Day / night
- Weekday mornings run on office time — Lalai Sap trading 06:30–15:00 and stall lunches peaking at midday; evenings belong to the skywalk (canal park lights after roughly 17:30 per one guide) and to rooftops, while the office core goes quiet. *[`chong-nonsi-c10`, `chong-nonsi-c14`, `chong-nonsi-c06`]*
- Sundays invert the unit: the same streets that carry the lunchtime crush are described as wide, quiet and slightly unsettling, and the park becomes the main still-functioning public space. *[`chong-nonsi-c14`, `chong-nonsi-c06`]*

## Within-area variation
- The Narathiwas/Sathorn junction is a tower canyon — fast-paced, limited greenery, highest condo density — while inner sois two turns off hold mature trees and older low-rise compounds; guides say rent street-by-street, not by postcode. *[`chong-nonsi-c18`]*
- The northern edge differs from the rest: Sala Daeng BTS (~730 m) and the Patpong/Silom entertainment streets (~657 m geotag) sit within a bare 800 m straight-line of the stop but outside the app's mapped catchment — the separately rated st.sala-daeng area takes them — so the unit's own nightlife density, not Patpong, carries its nightlife score. *[`chong-nonsi-c03`]*
- St Louis BTS, opened 2021 about 440 m southwest, is inside the circle but a separate station area with its own hospital-linked traffic — errands and apartment hunting should check which unit an address actually belongs to. *[`chong-nonsi-c04`, `chong-nonsi-c05`]*

## Conditional fit
- Choose this unit if your week is anchored at a Narathiwas/Sathorn office: one resident's 10-minute walk to work replaced an estimated 45-minute cross-town commute — the mechanism behind the area's whole value proposition. *[`chong-nonsi-c17`]*
- Avoid it if weekends are when you live: street-food-first eaters, weekend socializers and anyone needing Sunday conveniences nearby are explicitly screened out by the guides; go one stop north for that life. *[`chong-nonsi-c17`, `chong-nonsi-c14`]*
- Budget check before signing: one-bed medians near 24,000 THB are the honest baseline; two-bed 'typical' figures are composition-skewed, so price by unit size and building age, not by the station-area median. *[`chong-nonsi-c12`, `chong-nonsi-c13`]*

## Explicit unknowns
- Canal park opening hours: a 16:30–21:00 figure appeared in one search snippet and was never confirmed by a body-read source.
- Continuity of the opened park section: opened 25 Dec 2021, but a March 2024 site visit found walkways fenced off — whether it was closed, reopened, or the visit caught finishing works is unresolved.
- Phase 3 (Soi Narathiwas 7–Chan Rd) status in 2025–2026: unverified social-media update says unfinished in 2025 with completion hoped for 2026; no official source read.
- The app's '4.2 ha park within 800 m' could not be reconciled with any sourced area figure (total five-phase project ~70 rai ≈ 11 ha; opened section much smaller).
- The ~300,000 vehicles/day figure for Narathiwas Road is a critic's claim from 2021, never measured in a body-read source.
- Canal smell and flood history: only the 2021 'smelly canal' description exists; one 2026 guide explicitly declined to state flood risk for lack of government data.
- Patpong boundary geometry rests on a single block-level geotag; which bars specifically fall inside 800 m is unknown.
- One Bangkok and other mega-projects: outside the unit and unverified here; not asserted either way.
- No 2024–2026 safety or crime data for the unit was found; the only station-linked incident in sources is the minor 2019 bombing.
- Whether the park's accessibility criticisms (BRT wheelchair access, signalized crossings) were ever remedied is undocumented.
