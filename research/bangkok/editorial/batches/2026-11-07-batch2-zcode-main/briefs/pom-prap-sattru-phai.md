## Guide lead (proposed)
Pom Prap Sattru Phai is the thin residential skin between Bangkok's ceremonial centre and its trading core. Wikipedia's numbers make it the densest district in Thailand (about 24,100/km2 registered in 2017; the app uses the 2020 point, 41,524 people), and its landmarks read like a packing list: the Golden Mount in khwaeng Ban Bat, the 1900 Nang Loeng market in Wat Sommanat, the electronics lanes of Khlong Thom, the Soi Nana bar strip and the new Bangkok Kunsthalle in khwaeng Pom Prap, and the Rajadamnern boxing stadium on Ratchadamnoen. None of Bangkok's 12 Blue Line stations named for this area is administratively inside it - Wat Mangkon sits across Charoen Krung in Samphanthawong, Sam Yot across the moat in Phra Nakhon - which is the district in one sentence: it lives off edges. The honest counterweights are documented: its garment wholesale (Bobae) is contracting fast, and in August 2026 a fire in the steel-structured Khlong Thom Center injured 11 and partly collapsed the building - the dense-old-town tax, paid in public.

## Strengths
- Heritage that still trades: Nang Loeng (opened 1900, Bangkok's first land-based market) runs daily around century-old wooden shophouses, and the Michelin guide recognizes both the modern face (Potong's star on Soi Nana) and the traditional one (Bunloet's Bib Gourmand noodle stall near Nang Loeng). *[`pom-prap-sattru-phai-c07`, `pom-prap-sattru-phai-c17`]*
- Free-to-use open space where other districts have parks: the Golden Mount compound is publicly entered roughly 7:30am-7pm with a fee only on the 344-step climb, and the restored Khlong Ong Ang promenade (2020 UN-Habitat award) runs 1.5 km along both banks two-to-three minutes from Sam Yot MRT. *[`pom-prap-sattru-phai-c04`, `pom-prap-sattru-phai-c06`]*
- A nightlife register most central districts lack: small bars in shophouses (Tep with live Thai music Fri-Sun from 7pm) plus a muay thai stadium that holds fights every day of the week - no club strip required. *[`pom-prap-sattru-phai-c03`, `pom-prap-sattru-phai-c11`]*
- Asking rents that undercut central Bangkok for the location, at the verified low end at least: The Prague 1-beds listed at 12,000-13,000 THB against a 12,000-25,000 district band. *[`pom-prap-sattru-phai-c13`]*

## Trade-offs
- The wholesale base is shrinking under the character it creates: Bo Bae foot traffic is reported down up to 95% since e-commerce, with storefronts emptying even as some rents were slashed from 40,000 to 8,000 THB - the 'two clocks' of the district are not equally loud anymore. *[`pom-prap-sattru-phai-c08`, `pom-prap-sattru-phai-c14`]*
- Density is structural, not incidental: the most densely populated district in Thailand per Wikipedia, with crowding that changes register by hour - market morning, canal-walk Fri-Sun evening, bar night - and 13 nights of fairground saturation every November at the Golden Mount. *[`pom-prap-sattru-phai-c01`, `pom-prap-sattru-phai-c05`, `pom-prap-sattru-phai-c14`]*
- Old-town building risk is realized, not theoretical: the August 2026 Khlong Thom Center fire (11 injured, partial collapse of a steel-structured market building) shows what the shophouse-and-market fabric can cost. *[`pom-prap-sattru-phai-c10`]*
- A sex-industry pocket at 22 July Circle (per Wikipedia) sits between the temples and the craft bars - a vibe discontinuity the food and nightlife counts do not show. *[`pom-prap-sattru-phai-c16`]*

## Day / night
- Morning: Nang Loeng from about 6:00 (heirloom desserts, braised duck, beef noodles) while the electronics and phone lanes wind up; Golden Mount compound opens to its calmest hours. *[`pom-prap-sattru-phai-c07`, `pom-prap-sattru-phai-c04`, `pom-prap-sattru-phai-c14`]*
- Evening: Fri-Sun the Khlong Ong Ang promenade runs 16:00-22:00 with food stalls and lantern-lit alleys, Tep's live Thai music starts around 7pm on Soi Nana, and Rajadamnern's nightly cards fill the stadium. *[`pom-prap-sattru-phai-c06`, `pom-prap-sattru-phai-c03`, `pom-prap-sattru-phai-c11`]*
- November overrides the routine: the Golden Mount fair runs 13 days and nights (17-29 Nov in 2026) with a dawn red-cloth procession, rides and street food, and is described by Time Out as Bangkok's biggest temple fair. *[`pom-prap-sattru-phai-c05`]*

## Within-area variation
- The Wat Sommanat/Nang Loeng face is morning-weighted and residential-heritage (1900 market, Wat Sommanat, Bunloet's stall), while the khwaeng Pom Prap face at Soi Nana/Khlong Thom is trade-and-nightlife - the two sides of the khet keep different hours. *[`pom-prap-sattru-phai-c07`, `pom-prap-sattru-phai-c03`, `pom-prap-sattru-phai-c17`]*
- The edges work differently: the southern edge (Charoen Krung/Worachak) carries Khlong Thom's electronics trade and the August 2026 fire site; the western edge (the boundary canal) carries the promenade and Sam Yot access; the north (Khlong Phadung Krung Kasem at Nang Loeng) keeps the fort-era street grid. *[`pom-prap-sattru-phai-c10`, `pom-prap-sattru-phai-c06`, `pom-prap-sattru-phai-c07`]*
- Nightlife is two registers apart: craft-cocktail small bars on Soi Nana versus the 22 July Circle sex-industry pocket a few blocks away - both inside the khet, neither representative of the other. *[`pom-prap-sattru-phai-c03`, `pom-prap-sattru-phai-c16`]*
