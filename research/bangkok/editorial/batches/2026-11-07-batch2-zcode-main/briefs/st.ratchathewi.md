## Card preview (proposed)
> One BTS stop west of Siam's malls, Ratchathewi trades polish for proximity: dinner streets that open at 16:00 and 18:00 in old shophouse lanes, a canal-boat pier 305 m from the ticket gates, and asking rents several thousand baht below the neighboring rated areas - offset by a flyoverless intersection and three years of Orange Line construction works.

## Guide lead (proposed)
Ratchathewi is the point where the Sukhumvit line's tourist Bangkok thins out into a district of Phetchaburi Road apartments, hotel rows and neighborhood eating streets. The station sits just south of the Ratchathewi intersection; escalators drop onto Co-Co Walk's inexpensive bars, a skybridge reaches the Asia Hotel, and the Saen Saep express boat leaves from a pier 305 m away toward Pratunam and the Golden Mount. Two food streets anchor the evenings: Soi 5 (16:00-23:00, rice boxes at 25-30 baht) and Soi 10 (18:00 until 01:00-02:00, closed Mondays) in lanes of old two-storey shophouses. Independent 2025-2026 rent data brackets the app's 21,500/38,000 THB figures, below neighboring Siam. The tradeoffs are concrete: no named public park inside the catchment on the maps we checked, a broad arterial to cross on footbridges, and the intersection's flyover gone since January 2025 for the MRT Orange Line, due back around 2028.
