## Card preview (proposed)
> A station whose address is a mall: BTS Charoen Nakhon (charoen-nakhon-c01) opens by bridge directly into ICONSIAM and the ICS complex across the road (charoen-nakhon-c05, c08), while the streets behind it are old Khlong San lanes that lost their 40-year market in 2021 (charoen-nakhon-c12). The river is the area's second transit system — 12+ mapped piers on both banks within 800 m, including east-bang Rak ones across the bend (charoen-nakhon-c09) — but its scheduled boat service is thinner than the pier count implies (charoen-nakhon-c10).

## Guide lead (proposed)
Charoen Nakhon is two neighborhoods stapled together by a 300-metre walkway. One is ICONSIAM: a 525,000 m2 riverfront complex with Sook Siam's all-province food hall, a >10,000 m2 riverside terrace and the nightly fountain show, wired to the Gold Line's G2 station by covered bridges (charoen-nakhon-c07, c05). The other is inland Khlong San: Wat Suwan's temple lanes, the district office, a Lotus's at the station's doorstep, and the fenced-off block where Khlong San Plaza market traded for four decades until its SRT lease ran out in 2021 (charoen-nakhon-c12, c13). The Gold Line (three stops, no river crossing, flat fare) is the only rail, and its ridership of 1,809-8,390/day shows most people reach the city by bus or the mall's free boat instead (charoen-nakhon-c02, c03).

## Strengths
- Covered, weather-proof walk from three station exits straight into ICONSIAM and ICS; the mall is entered from the station exits over short direct bridges - steps, not a hike (charoen-nakhon-c05, c06) *[`charoen-nakhon-c05`, `charoen-nakhon-c06`]*
- Unusually dense river access for a single station area: ICONSIAM, Wat Suwan, Khlong San, Hilton and Taksin piers on the home bank plus Si Phraya/Oriental/CAT piers across the bend (charoen-nakhon-c09) *[`charoen-nakhon-c09`]*
- Eight BMTA bus routes at the station street level, including Talat Phlu and Ekkamai links that bypass the Gold Line (charoen-nakhon-c11) *[`charoen-nakhon-c11`]*
- Everyday anchor within 100 m: a Lotus's supermarket plus the ICS retail complex across the road from the station (charoen-nakhon-c08, c13) *[`charoen-nakhon-c08`, `charoen-nakhon-c13`]*
- Sook Siam's all-77-provinces food hall and a free nightly river fountain show give the area an outsized food-and-evening offer for its size (charoen-nakhon-c07, c17) *[`charoen-nakhon-c07`, `charoen-nakhon-c17`]*

## Trade-offs
- No direct BTS/MRT: one feeder shuttle to the Silom line at Krung Thon Buri, and its under-9,000/day ridership shows the transfer cost is real (charoen-nakhon-c02, c03) *[`charoen-nakhon-c02`, `charoen-nakhon-c03`]*
- The traditional local market closed end-2021 after the SRT lease expired — vendors displaced, the site fenced for redevelopment, and the cross-river ferry pier's contract lapsed the same December (charoen-nakhon-c12) *[`charoen-nakhon-c12`]*
- Mall-hours dependence: retail/dining pulse runs 10:00-22:00 with packed weekend evenings and calm weekday mornings; the street scene after dark is thin (charoen-nakhon-c17) *[`charoen-nakhon-c17`]*
- The mapped '9 piers' include hotel, private and closed facilities; express boats serve the area only via stops the operator does not even list publicly (charoen-nakhon-c10) *[`charoen-nakhon-c10`]*
- The only named municipal park in the 800 m circle cannot be confidently identified — the prime candidate's own coordinates conflict (charoen-nakhon-c16) *[`charoen-nakhon-c16`]*

## Day / night
- Weekday morning: calm at the mall and on the lanes; morning-market trading lingers near the old Khlong San Plaza site; express boats and buses run, Gold Line quietest (charoen-nakhon-c17, c12, c03) *[`charoen-nakhon-c17`, `charoen-nakhon-c12`, `charoen-nakhon-c03`]*
- Weekday evening: fountain show on the River Park terrace, hotel bars and dinner cruises; mall shuts at 22:00 and the streets quiet with it (charoen-nakhon-c17, c07) *[`charoen-nakhon-c17`, `charoen-nakhon-c07`]*
- Weekend evening: the fountain terrace crowds up enough that guides say arrive 30+ minutes early; boats and Gold Line run past the show, but express-boat lines are Monday-Friday only (charoen-nakhon-c17, c10) *[`charoen-nakhon-c17`, `charoen-nakhon-c10`]*

## Within-area variation
- Riverside strip: supertall condo towers (Magnolias ~318 m, Mandarin Oriental Residences ~272 m) and hotel riverfront — the postcard version (charoen-nakhon-c15) *[`charoen-nakhon-c15`]*
- Station block: ICS mall, Lotus's, bus stop, college — functional, walkway-connected (charoen-nakhon-c08, c11, c13) *[`charoen-nakhon-c08`, `charoen-nakhon-c11`, `charoen-nakhon-c13`]*
- Inland lanes (Wat Suwan, Soi Somdet Chao Phraya): low-rise community blocks at 16,000-24,500 THB 1-bed rents, a short walk from 200,000+ river-tower listings (charoen-nakhon-c14, c15) *[`charoen-nakhon-c14`, `charoen-nakhon-c15`]*
- Across the river bend: the Si Phraya/Oriental pier strip and Talat Noi's shophouse quarter sit 500-900 m straight-line away — inside the catchment only if the app counts river-crossed ground (charoen-nakhon-c09, c13) *[`charoen-nakhon-c09`, `charoen-nakhon-c13`]*

## Conditional fit
- Good fit if your life can route through ICONSIAM/ICS: mall dining, riverside running, covered station-home walks (charoen-nakhon-c07, c05) *[`charoen-nakhon-c07`, `charoen-nakhon-c05`]*
- Workable if you commute by bus or boat; eight bus routes and dense piers compensate for the feeder-only rail (charoen-nakhon-c11, c09) *[`charoen-nakhon-c11`, `charoen-nakhon-c09`]*
- Poor fit if you need direct BTS/MRT, street-level nightlife after 22:00, or wet-market everyday shopping — the market closed in 2021 and the street scene follows mall hours (charoen-nakhon-c02, c17, c12) *[`charoen-nakhon-c02`, `charoen-nakhon-c17`, `charoen-nakhon-c12`]*
- Families with a hospital dependency should check options first: only a psychiatric institute is confirmed in-catchment (charoen-nakhon-c13) *[`charoen-nakhon-c13`]*

## Claim index

- `charoen-nakhon-c01` **[proposed·strength·entire_unit]** The unit's physical stop is BTS Charoen Nakhon, station code G2 on the BTS Gold Line, at 13.7265N/100.5090E on Charoen Nakhon Road, Khlong San district (west/Thonburi bank of the Chao Phraya). It opened 16 December 2020, is owned by the BMA and operated by Krungthep Thanakom (with BTSC under a 30-year O&M contract), and its street level includes a bus stop, Mitr Phol Business Administration Technological College, Millennium Hilton Hotel and Magnolias Waterfront Residences. _(scope: venue — BTS Charoen Nakhon (Gold Line G2))_
- `charoen-nakhon-c02` **[proposed·neutral·entire_unit]** The Gold Line is a ~1.74-1.8 km elevated automated people mover with three stations in sequence: G1 Krung Thon Buri (interchange with the BTS Silom line), G2 Charoen Nakhon (ICONSIAM), G3 Khlong San (terminus; future SRT connection). It runs entirely on the Thonburi (west) side and does NOT cross the Chao Phraya river, contradicting the premise that the station connects 'across the river' — the BTS network link itself is the interchange at G1. Service opened free on 16 Dec 2020, charged 15 THB from mid-Jan 2021; Wikipedia currently lists a flat 16 THB while third-party sites list 17 THB; rides were free again 25-31 Jan 2025 under the PM2.5 policy. _(scope: corridor — BTS Gold Line Krung Thon Buri - Khlong San)_
- `charoen-nakhon-c03` **[corroborated·tradeoff·entire_unit]** Gold Line usage is small and mall-dominated: in the free opening month it averaged 5,000-6,000 passengers/day, of whom ~3,000 used the ICONSIAM (Charoen Nakhon) station; the line fell to 1,809/day in Q1 2022 and grew to 8,390/day by Q4 2025 (7,885 in Q3 2026). Press summaries put the pre-opening forecast at ~42,000/day and a later share of ~0.44% of Bangkok mass-transit trips; the free shuttle boat to Sathorn is widely cited as competing with the fare-charging line. _(scope: corridor — BTS Gold Line)_
- `charoen-nakhon-c04` **[corroborated·neutral·partial]** Planned extensions would push the Gold Line ~1 km further along Somdet Chao Phraya Road to connect with the future MRT Purple Line southern extension, then ~0.95 km more west to a Prajadhipok terminus; as of 2025-2026 Thai-language transit commentary the construction has not been approved/started, and a future G4 'Prachathipok' station is drawn between Soi Somdet Chao Phraya 6-8. Until then the unit keeps no direct second-rail-line link. _(scope: corridor — Somdet Chao Phraya Road corridor)_
- `charoen-nakhon-c05` **[corroborated·strength·inside]** The station is stitched to the mall by elevated walkways: Exit 1 opens toward the ICS building and Sois Charoen Nakhon 2/4, Exit 2 connects through the 'Charoen Nakhon Hall' bridge, and Exit 3 serves ICONSIAM's main building plus Mandarin Oriental residences, the Hilton and Sois 3/5 — so the mall is a 2-5 minute covered walk, not an 800 m trek. _(scope: venue — BTS Charoen Nakhon exits / ICONSIAM links)_
- `charoen-nakhon-c06` **[corroborated·neutral·inside]** ICONSIAM is effectively the unit's southern half-anchor: the OSM mall polygon center sits ~0.14 km from the station, the complex's southern end (~13.722, 100.5102, the River Park area the app briefing cites) lies ~0.51 km straight-line, and every rated neighbor (BTS Krung Thon Buri ~1.2 km WSW, Wongwian Yai ~2 km WSW, Talat Phlu farther SW) is farther for that ground, so no plausible nearest-stop clip removes it. - `charoen-nakhon-c07` **[corroborated·strength·inside]** ICONSIAM is a 525,000 m2 riverfront mega-mall opened 9 November 2018 (Siam Piwat + MQDC + CP, ~THB 54bn): Siam Takashimaya, Thailand's riverside Apple store, ICONLUXE luxury wing, the River Museum, two supertall residential towers (Magnolias Waterfront Residences ~318 m/70 floors; Mandarin Oriental Residences ~272 m), and a riverside park of more than 10,000 m2 with a public riverside walkway; four piers on site serve private boats and public ferries, with visitor ferries from Sathorn, Sheraton and Si Phraya piers. _(scope: venue — ICONSIAM complex)_
