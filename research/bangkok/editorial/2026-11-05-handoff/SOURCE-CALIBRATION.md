# Source calibration — read before starting the pilot

These are current-task web reads from **5 November 2026**, not completed or
approved entity profiles. They show why the research needs separate evidence,
scope and editorial review.

| Source actually read | Useful signal | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Time Out: Thonglor](https://www.timeout.com/bangkok/things-to-do/your-ultimate-guide-to-thonglor), 14 May 2026, Tita Honghirunkham | The writer emphasizes dining, bars, music and wellness, and describes Soi 55 plus branching streets. This is useful editorial character material. | That every mentioned venue lies in `st.thong-lo`'s 800 m catchment; a resident consensus; current asking rents; or a verified route. Even directional statements need a map check. |
| [Time Out: Ari](https://www.timeout.com/bangkok/things-to-do/ari), 30 May 2026, Kraprib Premsinee and Kaweewat Siwanartwong | A positive account of cafes, independent shops, residential side streets and a gentler rhythm; it also discusses gentrification. | That Ari is quiet on every street or at every hour, that leafy streets equal accessible parks, or that the publication's enthusiasm is an app rating. |

Rent bands have no demonstrated sample in the inspected page. Record commercial/editorial context and corroborate consequential claims. |

The seed's **Thong Lo 6.46** and **Ari 7.28** are independently computed raw
app composites under the recorded default weights. Their displayed values
are 6.5 and 7.3. A favorable guide does not override these numbers, and a lower
model score does not invalidate a writer's reported preferences. Research must
explain the difference in objective, geography, time and trade-offs.

News discovery also demonstrated two rejection paths:

- A canonical AP article URL returned an access error in the current read.
  Its search snippet was not promoted to verified news evidence.
- A result presenting itself as Bangkok Post was served by an unrelated
  `workers.dev` proxy. It was not used or fetched as an authorized original.
  Locate the canonical publisher or an independently accessible original.

No news-derived station-level flood claim is approved by this calibration.
The research agent still needs current, geographically specific news and
official corroboration where appropriate. A general Bangkok event cannot be
turned into a persistent risk label for every station area.

The first-pass image work is intentionally only a plan. Publisher photos and
credits in these articles are not blanket permission to download or reuse them.
