# Tokyo day/night noise — machine-readability of Japanese sources (CRTKY-175)

Researched: **2026-10-13**, by the open-data research agent. Companion to the
Bangkok noise ticket **CRTKY-151**
(`research/bangkok/notes/2026-10-13-noise-crtky151.md`, read in full before
this research) — the ask asked for the same question form and a shared-pattern
comparison (§9). All fetches below were run by me in this session; commands
and outputs are quoted. Nothing here is an implementation decision — the owner
adopts sources.

**TL;DR:** Unlike Bangkok (5 live stations, daily-24 h only, no day/night
buildable), **Tokyo has a measured day/night noise layer that is actually
machine-readable**: TMG's annual vehicle-traffic-noise survey publishes
**昼間 (day 06–22 h) and 夜間 (night 22–06 h) Leq per measurement point** —
267 routine points in FY2023 (CSV + Excel directly on kankyo.metro.tokyo.lg.jp)
and **673 points in FY2013 with lat/lon in the open-data catalog**
(14 years FY2000–2013, CSV, CC BY 4.0). Ward-level **noise-complaint counts**
are also machine-readable (Tokyo Statistical Yearbook table 19-11, 49
municipalities × 5 years, CSV). What does NOT exist publicly: hourly or
continuous monitoring — so **hourly resolution is impossible; an annual
day/night split at fixed roadside points is not**. まちもり (My City Report)
is not a noise source (verified). Verdict for the ticket: **promising** —
adopt the TMG day/night points as measured anchors (station-level
annotations + model calibration), build station-level day/night quietness as
**modeled** (nightlife + arterials + 首都高 ramps) calibrated by these
points; do **not** promise hourly noise.

## 2. MAIN FIND — TMG 自動車交通騒音調査結果 (vehicle traffic noise survey)

Tokyo's Bureau of Environment (東京都環境局) runs an annual roadside noise
survey with two point sets: **常時監視測定地点** (routine monitoring points)
and **要請限度測定地点** (requested-limit points, 車騒法 article-16
request-limit roads). Both are published **with explicit day and night Leq**.

### 2.1 Newest year: FY2023 (令和5年度) — verified live

Page `https://www.kankyo.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/vehicle/noise/result/cyousakekka`
(fetched 2026-10-13) lists exactly five year pages — **令和元年度 through
令和5年度 (FY2019–FY2023)**; the parent page
`/vehicle/noise` carries a "last updated 2023-11-01" footer. FY2023 files,
all HTTP 200 via `curl`:

| File | URL (`https://www.kankyo.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/documents/d/kankyo/` +) | Format | Content verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| 令和５年度_調査結果（エクセル） | `-_-28` | XLSX 110,449 B | 3 sheets: 項目説明 / 常時監視測定地点(R05年度) / 要請限度測定地点(R05年度) — 276/297/371 rows |
| 項目説明 | `-_-3` | CSV 4,399 B | field dictionary; **"昼間の定義 16時間（午前6時から午後10時）/ 夜間の定義 8時間（午後10時から翌午前6時）"**; 道路種別 legend "１：高速自動車国道　２：首都高速道路　３：一般国道　４：都道　５：４車線以上の区市町村道　６：その他道路" |
| 常時監視測定地点 | `-_-27` | CSV 28,728 B, **CP932** | 267 data rows, all with day+night Leq (§2.2) |
| 要請限度測定地点 | `-_-29` | CSV 34,376 B, CP932 | 369 data rows: day/night Leq + **振動 day/night + traffic volume day/night (台/10分)**, e.g. row 1 都道白山祝田田町線: Leq 70/67, vibration 44/42, traffic 97/33 |

A PDF overview (令和５年度_調査結果の概要, `-_-04_-p1-p4-`) also exists; I did
not parse it (the CSVs supersede it).

### 2.2 What the FY2023 常時監視 CSV actually contains (parsed)

Header (row 2): `評価対象道路①(路線名) / 車線数 / 道路種別 / 遮音壁 / 低騒音舗装 /
評価対象道路② / 測定開始年月日 / 測定終了年月日 / 車道端からの距離(m) /
道路敷地境界からの距離(m) / 地上高さ(m) / (昼間)Leq / (夜間)Leq` — plus
測定地点 address, 用途地域 code 1–7, 環境基準類型 AA/A/B/C.

- **267 points, 267 with both day and night Leq.**
- Day Leq: min 54, median **66**, mean 65.9, max 74. Night Leq: min 48,
  median **62**, mean 62.3, max 73. Day−night gap: median **3 dB**, max 12 dB.
- Against the **road-facing** environmental standards (環告64: A region on a
  ≥2-lane road 60/55; B/C region on a lane-equipped road 65/60 — recomputed
  per point from the CSV's own 環境基準類型 + 車線数 columns): **63 % of
  points exceed by day, 74 % by night** (169/267 and 198/267). These are
  roadside points, so non-attainment is the norm — consistent with the
  national report treating road-facing areas as the worst category.
- 道路種別 of the 267: 都道 193, 一般国道 49, 区市町村道(4+) 12,
  **首都高速 8**, 高速自動車国道 5. 環境基準類型: A 59, B 79, C 129.
- **144 of 267 points are in the 23 special wards** (Minato 11, Nerima 10,
  Chuo 9, Ota 9, Sumida 8, Adachi 8, Katsushika 8, Edogawa 7, Koto 7,
  Suginami 7 … Shibuya 5, Setagaya 4, Arakawa 2); the rest cover Tama cities
  and the island/western towns.
- Shibuya-ku (5 points, all arterial): 渋谷区神宮前5丁目 国道246号 68/66,
  鉢山町 環状6号線 67/66, 広尾 67/65, 本町 角筈和泉線 65/61,
  東3丁目 区道887号 66/64 → ward average **66.6 day / 64.4 night**.
- Setagaya-ku (4 points): 野毛 大田調布線 62/58, 用賀 **首都高3号渋谷線**
  64/63, 玉川 多摩堤通り 66/61, 岡本 東名高速 65/64.
- **No measurement point in 千歳船橋** — and none on any residential street
  anywhere; the network is by construction an arterial network.
- **No lat/lon columns in FY2023** — address only
  (e.g. `渋谷区神宮前5丁目53-1`); geocoding needed (the FY2013 release has
  coordinates, §2.3).

### 2.3 Archive on the TMG open-data catalog: FY2000–FY2013, CSV, with lat/lon

CKAN search `catalog.data.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/api/3/action/package_search?q=騒音`
→ **52 datasets**; 15 belong to 東京都環境局, of which **14 are the annual
自動車交通騒音調査結果 for 平成12–25 (FY2000–FY2013)**, e.g.
`平成25年度 自動車交通騒音調査結果` (`name=t000009d1900000003`, 80 resources:
1 summary + 28 per-ward CSVs + per-road CSVs, URLs like
`https://www.opendata.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/kankyo/vehicle/noise/H25/H25_kekka.csv`).
**License of all 14: CC BY 4.0** (`license_id` in the CKAN package;
`metadata_modified` 2025-12-12).

FY2013 summary CSV `H25_kekka.csv` parsed (CP932):
`一連番号,測定地点住所,緯度,経度,座標系(JGD2011),道路名,環境基準類型,測定開始年月日,測定終了年月日,道路種別,車線数,車道端からの距離,地上からの高さ,昼間等価騒音レベル(dB),夜間等価騒音レベル(dB)` —
**673 points, 671 with day+night; day mean 67.6 / night mean 64.5 / gap 3.1**.
Decade comparison: FY2013 67.6/64.5 → FY2023 65.9/62.3 (−1.7 / −2.2 dB,
consistent series, same semantics).

Setagaya ward file `H25_kushichouson_12.csv`: 25 points, all on arterials
(環七, 玉川通り, 環八, 甲州街道, 目黒通り, 世田谷通り, 赤堤通り, 多摩堤通り…);
**none in 千歳船橋** — nearest measured point ≈1.7 km (砧公園7 環八通り,
35.632, 139.624; station at 35.6476, 139.6245). Confirms the network shape is
stable across years.

**Catalog–site gap (honest):** the open-data catalog series stops at FY2013;
the TMG site carries FY2019–FY2023 as Excel/CSV. **FY2014–FY2018 were not
found in either place** (the 年度別 page lists only 令和1–5; catalog has
平成12–25) — I did not probe every URL, so treat "missing" as "not found on
the two landing pages I fetched".

## 3. 東京都環境白書2025 (Environment White Paper) — noise slice, with a data-quality caveat

Dataset `東京都環境白書2025` (`t000009d2000000066`, CC BY 4.0, updated
2026-03-31, 39 CSV resources). Noise-relevant, fetched and inspected:

- `34_shinkansen_noise_2024.csv` — correct: 22 point rows (Tokaido
  Shinkansen, Shinagawa/Ota wards), 12.5/25/50 m levels + attainment;
  "環境基準達成状況 24／31（77％）".
- **`35_aircraft_noise_2024.csv` is broken** — its 1,577 bytes are a copy of
  `36_air_quality_standards.csv` (大気汚染に係る環境基準); the actual aircraft
  noise table is not in the CSV release. Resource content is shifted/misnamed
  for at least this one file.
- **`39_noise_standards.csv` is broken too** — it contains 温室効果ガス排出量
  (GHG emissions), not the noise standards table.

So the White Paper CSV set is not a reliable noise source until fixed;
the Shinkansen file alone is useful (Shinkansen is explicitly excluded from
the general noise standards anyway).

## 4. 環境省 (national MoE) — PDF only, no machine-readable points

- Portal `https://www.env.go.jp/air/noise/` (HTTP 200): publishes the annual
  騒音規制法等施行状況調査 as **PDF reports only** — e.g. 令和６年度
  (FY2024) 概要 656 KB (`/content/000384661.pdf`) + 報告書 9.98 MB
  (`/content/000384688.pdf`), back to 平成25. Parsed the FY2024 概要 with
  pypdf: national noise complaints **19,886 (FY2024) / 19,890 (FY2023)**;
  **東京都 3,981 = the largest of any prefecture**; attainment: 287
  municipalities measured, 2,315 points, **90.3 % attainment**. The 別紙
  達成状況 PDF (`/content/900517174.pdf`, 9 pages) adds that only ~25 % of
  type-designated municipalities even measure annually (315 of 1,242 in
  FY2019; 2,628 points incl. 2,000 定点). **No CSV/Excel anywhere on these
  pages; per-point data stays with prefectures.**
- The legal day/night definition comes from the standards page
  `https://www.env.go.jp/kijun/oto1-1.html` (official 環告64 text, fetched):
  **昼間 = 06:00–22:00, 夜間 = 22:00–06:00**; general-area standards
  AA 50/40, A·B 55/45, C 60/50 dB; road-facing areas have a separate table
  (A+2-lane road: 60/55 etc.); "夜間の騒音レベルが７３デシベルを超える住居等
  が存する地域" is the priority-remediation trigger. **These are exactly the
  time windows the TMG survey uses** (§2.1) — the semantics match our
  "day/night" question natively.

## 5. Complaints (苦情) — ward-level annual counts are machine-readable

This is the closest Tokyo analog of Traffy's เสียงรบกวน stream — but
aggregated, not geo-tagged, and annual:

- **東京都統計年鑑 table 19-11 公害の種類別苦情件数** — direct CSV:
  `https://www.toukei.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/tnenkan/2024/tn24qv191100.csv`
  (HTTP 200, UTF-8, bilingual header, `地域コード` present), dataset
  東京都統計年鑑 令和6年 19B 環境 (`t000003d2000001166`, CC BY 4.0), same
  table for 令和2–6 (FY2020–FY2024). Structure: rows = 年度 × 地域
  (0=東京都, 1=区部/市部…, 2=each of 49 区市町村), columns include **騒音
  Noise** (plus air/water/soil/vibration/odor). FY2024 extract:
  Tokyo total 7,638 complaints of which **3,726 noise**; ku-area 2,849;
  most-complained wards (noise, FY2024): 足立 243, 墨田 239, **渋谷 216**
  (71 % of all Shibuya complaints!), 江戸川 192, 港 185, 大田 183;
  世田谷 123; quietest: 文京 4 (full table in the CSV).
- **Ward-level files with source breakdown** — e.g. 大田区 `公害に関する苦情`
  XLSX (`131113_R7_33_kougainikansurukujou.xlsx`, CC BY 4.0): FY2024 monthly
  counts, **騒音 184 of 368 complaints (50 %)**, and a 発生源別 table:
  noise by source = 建設作業 79, その他 86, 工場 14, 指定作業場 5. 目黒区
  (XLSX), 調布市 (XLS) have analogs. **No ward splits "karaoke" or "life
  noise" as separate categories** — その他 (other) absorbs them; the
  karaoke-specific split the ask hoped for does not exist in what I found.
- 狛江市 `公害苦情受付件数` CSV **is misnamed** — the file I fetched
  (`132195_R5kankesan04.csv`) contains 特定施設 notification counts, not
  complaints (a second data-quality caveat to log alongside §3).
- **警視庁 (Metropolitan Police): not probed in this session.** Complaints
  about neighborhood noise in Tokyo are handled mostly by ward environmental
  offices (the sources above) rather than police, so the ward files are
  likely the right granularity anyway; whether MPD publishes a
  騒音苦情-by-署 table remains an open question (§11.3).

Сэтагая") matches the small counts.

signal — **274 vs 9 venues at 800 m (30×)** — while the arterial/首都高 counts
are the day-noise signal: Shibuya station sits inside a 63-way primary/trunk
mesh *and* 83 motorway/motorway-link ways of the 首都高 corridor within
2 km, whereas 千歳船橋's first motorway segment is beyond 2 km (49 ways at
3 km, 首都高 3号渋谷線/東名 direction). The measured TMG anchors bracket the
two ends: roadside arterial 65–68 dB day / 61–66 night; 首都高 elevated
64/63; quiet residential pocket (世田谷 野毛, small arterial) 62/58. The
model must separate "on an arterial" from "behind the first building row" —
the TMG data covers only the former.

## 7. まちもり / My City Report — not a noise source (verified)

The Tokyo analog of Traffy Fondue is **My City Report (MCR)**
(`https://www.mycityreport.jp`, run by 東京都建設局; TMG intro page
`kensetsu.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/about/pr/mcr`). Scope: **road/park/river damage
reporting via phone camera + GPS**. Fetched both pages 2026-10-13:
`騒音` occurs **0 times** on the TMG page; the mycityreport.jp site offers no
open-data dump (only commercial RoadManager damage-detection data terms).
**Verdict: not a noise source and not open — matches the ask's suspicion
("скорее всего нет").** Tokyo's complaint-signal analog is the 年鑑 19-11 CSV
(§5), which is open but annual.

## 8. Verdict on "is there hourly / day-night at all?"

- **Hourly / continuous: NO.** No Tokyo public source publishes a continuous
  or hourly noise feed — the TMG survey is a 2-day measurement campaign per
  point per year (測定開始/終了 dates ~24–72 h apart), aggregated into one
  day-Leq and one night-Leq per point. (Compare Bangkok CRTKY-151: even a
  *latest-hour* snapshot existed there, but no hourly history either.)
- **Day/night: YES — measured, explicit, machine-readable.**
  `昼間等価騒音レベル(dB)` and `夜間等価騒音レベル(dB)` per point, 267 points
  FY2023 (+369 要請限度 points with vibration and traffic), 673 points FY2013
  with coordinates, 14 archive years on the catalog. Definition 06–22/22–06
  matches MoE 環告64.
- **Geographic coverage: arterials only.** 100 % of points sit on
  highway-class roads (that is the survey's legal purpose: 車騒法
  request-limit roads and 環境基準 road-facing areas). Residential-street
  quietness — the thing a station like 千歳船橋 needs — is **not measured
  anywhere**; general-area (普通騒音) monitoring by wards is sporadic and
  uncoordinated nationally (§4: only ~25 % of municipalities measure in a
  given year).

## 9. Bangkok comparison (shared pattern with CRTKY-151)

| | Bangkok (CRTKY-151) | Tokyo (this note) |
|---|---|---|
| Measured network | PCD: 7 BKK stations, 5 live | TMG: 267 routine + 369 request-limit points/yr |
| Day/night in public data | **no** (24 h Leq + one latest-hour sample) | **yes** (昼間/夜間 Leq per point, legal definition) |
| Hourly history | no | no |
| Archive depth | daily 2016–2025 (1 station leaks 2014–2025) | annual point tables 2000–2013 (catalog, lat/lon) + 2019–2023 (site) |
| Complaints | Traffy เสียงรบกวน — **gated** (403/Cloudflare) | 年鑑 19-11 per-ward annual counts — **open CSV** (CC BY 4.0) |
| License | Open Data Common; file host unreachable; **expired TLS cert** | **CC BY 4.0**; hosts respond cleanly (no cert games) |
| Verdict there | partial; night half = modeled only | **promising**; night half still modeled off-arterial, but day/night anchors exist |

Shared pattern: **both cities measure only where roads are; residential
night-quietness is a modeled quantity in both.** The difference is that Tokyo
gives you (a) real day/night pairs to calibrate with, (b) complaint counts as
a second ward-level signal, and (c) a license and infrastructure that work
from this machine — no `curl -k`, no mirror roulette (Overpass flakiness
notwithstanding, §6).

## 10. Composition recommendation (for the owner to decide)

1. **Adopt (measured, point level):** FY2023 CSV+Excel (and the FY2013
   catalog series for coordinates + 14-year history) → station/area
   **annotations** ("TMG roadside monitor 300 m from this station: day 66 /
   night 64 dB") and **calibration anchors** for the modeled layer. Encode
   once: CP932 decoding + the `-_-NN` document URLs are stable.
2. **Build (modeled):** station-level day/night quietness = f(nightlife
   density @800 m, `highway=primary|trunk` proximity, 首都高 ramp/motorway
   proximity, HP `midnight_count` already in NocoDB) — tagged `modeled` /
   `estimate`; calibrate by sign+rank against the 267 points (arterial
   stations ≈ 65–68/61–66; the model must separate "on an arterial" from
   "behind the first building row", which the TMG data does NOT cover).
3. **Adopt (complaints, ward level):** 年鑑 19-11 CSV as a ward-level noise
   complaint signal (5 years already, one URL pattern, CC BY 4.0). Ward →
   station mapping already exists (`station_wards`, 100 % coverage).
4. **Reject:** claiming hourly or residential-street noise; interpolating
   the roadside points into a residential surface (same reject as Bangkok
   §5 of CRTKY-151); the broken 白書 noise resources (§3) until TMG fixes
   them.
5. **Keep on the backlog:** FY2014–2018 survey years (ask TMG 環境局 where
   they live); a bulk export request to TMG would also settle whether
   ward-side 普通騒音 (non-road) measurements exist in structured form.
