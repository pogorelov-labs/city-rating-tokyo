# Tokyo flood: replacing the SRTM<5 m proxy with official hazard zones (CRTKY-171)

Researched: **2026-10-13**, by the open-data research agent. Unblocks the Tokyo
half of the flood-dealbreaker work (Bangkok half is `research/bangkok/notes/2026-10-13-flood-crtky139.md`).
All checks below were run by me in this session; commands and live output are
quoted. Nothing here is an implementation decision — the owner adopts sources.

**TL;DR:** the ticket's headline source, MLIT **A51 内水浸水想定**, turns out to
be nearly empty for Tokyo: the 2025 Tokyo file (139 kB, CC BY 4.0) contains
**one municipality (福生市/Fussa, code 13218), 194 polygons, and 0 of 854
in-bbox stations inside a polygon**. It is REJECT as a Tokyo rating input.
The workhorse replacement is the **TMG 浸水予想区域図** (CKAN dataset
`t000014d0000000029`, 東京都建設局, CC BY 4.0): **14 river-basin zones as CSV
point grids at ~9 m × 11 m** (`図郭No,浸水深,地盤高,緯度,経度`), explicitly
covering **both** river (外水) and drainage/inland (内水) flooding — the exact
signal the SRTM<5 m elevation badge approximates today. Spot-checked two zones
(神田川: 1,099,858 points, 142 of 206 in-bbox stations with ≥0.3 m depth within
~500 m; 中川・綾瀬川: 1,042,618 points, 42/186). MLIT **A31b** river-flood
assumption areas (1次メッシュ 5339 = the whole project bbox, CC BY 4.0) work as
the secondary/coverage source, scenario layer **想定最大規模** (`A31b_201`);
its 計画規模 layer for "other rivers" was downloaded and parsed (20,080
polygons, depth ranks 1–4). All three sources are freely licensed; scraper
pins (SHA-256) and coverage gates are sketched in §6.

## 0. What is being replaced (current proxy)

`scripts/export-environment.py:35-45` — station elevations (Open-Elevation API
→ NocoDB `station_elevation`) are bucketed by `ELEVATION_TIERS`, where
**<5 m → tier `"very_low"`** ("high flood risk (below/near sea level)"). The
badge renders in `app/src/components/NaturalEnvironment.tsx` (per CLAUDE.md:318,
"flood-risk warning <5m") and the flood dealbreaker checkbox lives in
`app/src/components/FilterPanel.tsx` (CLAUDE.md:325). It is a *topography*
proxy: a station 4.9 m above sea level on a plateau ridge and one in a river
floodplain get the same flag. Official hazard polygons replace "is it low"
with "does the authority expect this place to flood, how deep".

Station counts used throughout (computed from `data/stations.json` in this
session): 1,493 stations total; **615** with `prefecture == "13"` (Tokyo);
**607** Tokyo stations inside the project bbox (≈35.5–36.0 N, 139.2–139.9 E);
**854** stations of all prefectures inside the bbox (Saitama/Chiba/Kanagawa
border stations included — relevant because A31b covers them too).

files for Saitama (6.6 MB) and Kanagawa (3.38 MB) may be usable for the

## 2. TMG 浸水予想区域図 (CKAN `t000014d0000000029`) — ADOPT as primary for Tokyo

CKAN API (`curl -sL https://catalog.data.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/api/3/action/package_show?id=t000014d0000000029`,
fetched 2026-10-13, `success: true`):

- Title 浸水予想区域図; organization **東京都建設局** (TMG Bureau of Construction);
  **license_id `CC-BY-4.0`**; dataset `metadata_modified` 2025-12-12; the CSV
  files' HTTP Last-Modified is **2026-01-15** (checked for 3 resources).
- Description (quote, translated): TMG maps expected inundation areas for
  **all TMG-managed rivers (islands excluded) in 14 zones**, showing dangerous
  locations and expected depth, and — key for this ticket — *"浸水予想区域図では、
  川から水があふれることで浸水する現象（外水氾濫）と下水道管の能力を超えた雨水が
  窪地などにたまることで浸水する現象（内水氾濫）の両方を示しています"* — it shows
  **both river overflow AND inland/drainage flooding**. So this single product

- Exactly **14 zones** (the ask's number confirmed), each with a CSV
  「浸水深・地盤高データ」(inundation depth + ground elevation) and a PDF 図郭割
  (map-sheet index). Zone CSV URLs are
  `https://www.opendata.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/kensetsu/R3/shinsui_<zone>.csv`:
  kandagawa, sumidagawa (隅田川及び新河岸川), syakujiigawa (石神井川及び白子川),
  jyounantiku (城南地区), koutounaibu (江東内部), nogawa (野川・仙川・入間川・
  谷沢川・丸子川), kuromegawa (黒目川・落合川・柳瀬川・空堀川・奈良橋川),
  zanborigawa, sakaigawa (境川), nakagawa (中川・綾瀬川圏域), tsurumigawa,
  asakawa (浅川圏域・大栗川・三沢川), tamagawa (霞川・多摩川上流圏域),

- Overlaps are merged per mesh with the **higher depth rank winning**.
- 「東京都独自の施策として、洪水予報河川及び水位周知河川以外の河川からの外水氾濫

A31b is also what gives flood signal for the **~247 non-Tokyo in-bbox
stations** (Saitama/Chiba/Kanagawa) that TMG zones don't cover (except

## 4. Which layers feed what

- **Flood dealbreaker (per-station boolean/rank):** primary = TMG 14-zone CSVs,
  per station take `max_depth_m` = max 浸水深 of grid points within its 800 m
  catchment (or nearest grid point); A31b **想定最大規模** (`A31b_201`) depth
  rank for stations outside TMG zones (Saitama/Chiba/Kanagawa; and as a
  cross-check inside Tokyo for TMG-managed-river gaps). 計画規模
  (`A31b_101`) is the optimistic scenario — useful as a second tier ("floods
  even at planned-scale rain") but not as the dealbreaker input; 想定最大 is
  the resident-facing "how bad can it get here" layer. 家屋倒壊 (A31b_401)
  and 浸水継続時間 (A31b_301) are prose/tooltips, not rating inputs (v1).
- **Safety refinement:** plausible as a *correlation/annotation* only — the
  dealbreaker filter already encodes it; do not re-enter the safety score.
  (Same stance as the Bangkok note.)
- **Keep the elevation badge** as-is: SRTM<5 m stays an honest *topography*
  display, but the flood *checkbox* moves to official zones; a station outside
  all published zones must read "no official flood zone" — never "safe" (§5).

## 5. Honest caveats (keep in methodology)

1. **Official zones understate.** MLIT's own caveat (quoted §3): flooding
   occurs outside designated zones and deeper than assumed. TMG maps are
   assumptions for TMG-managed rivers, not guarantees. Absence of designation
   ≠ safety — the UI wording must not imply it.
2. **TMG rain scenario unverified.** The CKAN page does not state which
   rainfall scale the depth grids assume (the 水害ハザードマップ作成の手引き
   convention is 想定最大規模, but I did not find it in the fetched text).
   Verify against the PDF 図郭割 or TMG pages before wording the filter.
3. **Rank bands are coarse in A31b** (0.5/3/5/10 m breaks) vs continuous
   depths in TMG CSVs; don't mix scales without mapping to a common banding.
4. **Zone bboxes overlap** (Kanda/Nakagawa around lng 139.745–139.802) —
   aggregate with max, not sum. My station checks used a ~500 m rounded-grid
   radius, deliberately generous; implementation should use the 800 m
   catchments for consistency with `data/crime/station-safety.json`.
5. **Volume:** full adopt ≈ 14 CSVs (~350–500 MB) + A31b class-10 103 MB +
   class-20 30.6 MB ≈ **630 MB download**, ~1.5 GB unpacked. Local-cache
   model (like `.cache/crime/`) is required.

## 6. Scraper plan (ingest-crime doctrine)

Pattern to copy: `scripts/scrapers/ingest-crime-open-data.py` —
`SOURCES` dict with `{url, sha256, page, title, licence, attribution}` per
file (lines 80–135), `pinned` loader that downloads to a `.cache/` dir and
**exits FATAL on SHA-256 mismatch** (lines 156–168), and coverage asserts that
refuse to emit data when a prefecture's rows fall below ~99 % of the published
total (lines 547–549). Also its header comment "Updating to a newer year:
change YEAR and every URL/SHA-256/page list below" — refreshes are manual and
intentional.

New `scripts/scrapers/ingest-flood-tokyo.py`:

1. `SOURCES` — pin all 14 TMG CSVs (URLs from the CKAN package; SHA-256 of the
   two verified ones recorded in §2, the rest to be pinned on first run —
   same bootstrap approach as the crime scraper's pref files), plus
   `A31b-24_10_5339_GML.zip` and `A31b-24_20_5339_GML.zip` (pin recorded §3),
   licence `CC BY 4.0`, attribution 「出典：国土交通省「国土数値情報」（国土数値情報
   ダウンロードサイト）」/「出典：東京都建設局 浸水予想区域図（東京都オープンデータ
   カタログサイト）」.
2. Loader: `.cache/flood/`, download-if-missing, sha256 verify, FATAL on
   mismatch; stream-parse (Kanda CSV = 1.1 M rows; A31b GML class-10 file will
   be large — parse curves/surfaces with a streaming XML iterator, don't load
   DOM).
3. Coverage gates (fail loudly, emit nothing):
   - every TMG CSV: header exactly `図郭No,浸水深,地盤高,緯度,経度`, ≥100k good
     rows (observed floor for a zone is ~1.0 M for the two biggest; small
     zones will be lower — calibrate after first full run), grid step within
     tolerance of 0.0000833°/0.000125°, bbox intersecting the project bbox;
   - A31b zips: the five layer folders present; 計画規模 polygon count in the
     observed order of magnitude (20k for class-20 mesh 5339);
   - station output: ≥95 % of the 854 in-bbox stations get a non-null verdict
     (in-zone depth or "outside official zones"); spot checks `shinjuku` NOT
     ≥0.3 m in Kanda zone, `kameari`/`shibamata` ARE in Nakagawa (observed §2).
4. Output: `data/flood/station-flood.json` — `{slug: {max_depth_m, rank,
   zones: [...], scenario: "max-assumed", source: "tmg"|"mlit-a31b"}}` — plus
   `data/flood/sources.json` with url/sha256/bytes/licence/attribution, read
   by `compute-ratings.py`/`build-datamart.py` like `data/crime/station-safety.json`.
5. Refresh: TMG files Last-Modified 2026-01-15; MLIT annual releases (stamps
   2025-03-26). SHA bump = manual commit (same policy as crime YEAR).
