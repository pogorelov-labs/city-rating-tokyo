## overpass-bangkok.json, overpass-tokyo.json
- Source: OpenStreetMap via Overpass API (mirror overpass.kumi.systems; overpass-api.de returned 504 on this machine)
- Query: `nwr["office"="diplomatic"] + nwr["diplomatic"]` within city bbox
  (Bangkok 13.47,100.30,13.98,100.96 — repo `scripts/bangkok/static_data.py:195`;
   Tokyo 35.5,139.2,36.0,139.9 — same bbox as research note CRTKY-180)

- URL: https://overpass-api.de/ (+ mirrors)

## wikidata-bangkok.json, wikidata-tokyo.json, wikidata-operators.json
- Source: Wikidata Query Service (query.wikidata.org/sparql)
- Query: P31=Q3917681 (embassy) AND (P159 = wd:Q1861 Bangkok / wd:Q1490 Tokyo,
  direct or via P131* chain); supplementary pull of P137 (operator = represented
  country), P1027, P17 (host country), P625, P576
