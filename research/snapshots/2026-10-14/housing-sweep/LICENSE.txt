Bangkok housing sweep — per-khet OSM building/station counts
=============================================================

Acquired: 2026-10-14, by the housing-sweep data agent.
Method: Overpass API, one request per khet, seven `out count` blocks
(building=apartments / residential / dormitory / commercial / mixed_use;
building:levels>=20 via regex; railway=station with station~subway|light_rail|
monorail), server-side `area(3600000000 + relation_id)` filters. Method as
documented in research/bangkok/notes/2026-10-13-housing-crtky148.md section 2.

Caveats (carry over from the research note)
-------------------------------------------
- OSM building coverage of Bangkok is partial: every count is a *mapped*
  lower bound, not real-world stock. Zeros are mapping gaps (verified as such).
- towers_20plus is a lower bound by construction (Bangkok mappers tag no
  10-19-floor band; `building:levels` >= 20 regex only).
- stations_in_khet uses the station~subway|light_rail|monorail tag combination;
  OSM `station=*` sub-tag coverage is patchy in the core (note section 5) —
  indicative only, not shippable as a fact without normalization.
- Rows come from different dump timestamps (see CSV); do not sum across rows
  into a "single-date citywide" claim without restating snapshots.
