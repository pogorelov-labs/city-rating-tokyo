# v0.1.0 — City Rating: Tokyo + Bangkok

First tagged release, dated **2026-10-26**, of the existing City Rating product.
Release preparation adds documentation only; package versions and committed
datasets are unchanged.

## Highlights

- **Tokyo + Bangkok:** 1,493 Greater Tokyo stations; Bangkok's 50 districts,
  133 walkable station areas and 200 m grid, with rail overlays and hub commutes.
- **10 rating categories, EN/JA/RU:** adjustable weights, rent/commute and
  category dealbreakers, comparisons and localized detail pages.
- **Bangkok central rent provenance:** 10 DooAsset station-area asking-rent
  proxies at **estimate** confidence. ComparePanel distinguishes published
  bedroom medians, district blends and editorial district estimates.
- **Geofabrik OSM fetch code:** Thailand PBF acquisition with source hashes
  and bbox-crossing geometry handling; no full spatial refresh is published.
- Recent hardening includes Next.js 16.4.2, MCP authentication fixes, shared
  normalization/equivalence checks, data-integrity gates, typed i18n and UI tests.

## Data sources and attribution

Tokyo combines OpenStreetMap, HotPepper, MLIT railway/passenger data, police
open data, Suumo listings and e-Stat fallbacks. Bangkok combines OpenStreetMap
and Overture Places, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons, the WorldPop constrained
2020 district population model, ThaiFloodRisk flood inputs, editorial estimates
and the cited DooAsset rent aggregates. Source dates and confidence differ by
category and location; the release date does not make every input current.

OpenStreetMap is © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0); WorldPop and
ThaiFloodRisk inputs carry CC BY 4.0 attribution. Commons images retain their
individual author/license credits. Overture inputs have source-specific terms;
the additional attribution review for a future refresh remains pending. See
the [methodology](https://city-rating.pogorelov.dev/methodology) and
[source overview](https://github.com/pogorelov-labs/city-rating-tokyo/blob/v0.1.0/README.md#data-sources-high-level).

## Known limitations

- **The full Bangkok spatial data refresh is excluded.** Experimental refreshed
  output has **10 validation failures** and loses tourism, Michelin and embassy
  enrichments. Its remediation is scoped, not implemented; geometry/green
  anomalies, source pinning and attribution review are pending for a future
  refresh in a separate PR.
- The 10 rent proxies are **single-provider, all-size condominium asking-rent
  medians by bedroom**, not fixed-size samples, transaction prices or
  representative market estimates. Lease duration/short-stay exclusions are
  not established. Dates and sample sizes are shown; confidence stays estimate.
- **All district and grid rents remain editorial.** The other 123 station
  areas use district blends; Ari and Siam receive no invented proxy.
- Scores are relative within each city/level, and coverage varies. A Bangkok
  8 is not a Tokyo 8; finely drawn grid cells do not imply equally local rent
  or safety evidence.

## Deployment

The Next.js app builds static city pages from committed JSON and runs as a
standalone Docker image. **Coolify auto-deploys main**; this release requires
**no database migration** or manual deployment. A published tag does not prove
the automatic deployment completed; check production separately.
