# v0.2.0 — Station and district guides

Released 2026-10-31.

The redesigned guides cover every Greater Tokyo station, Bangkok station area
and Bangkok district: 1,676 places, available in English, Japanese and Russian.

## Easier comparisons

Rent and commute estimates lead each guide, followed by a single category
comparison plot. Compare a place with the current median for the same city and
area type, or choose a nearby area. Open a category to inspect its inputs and
evidence quality.

Commute bars make destinations easier to compare. Area maps show Bangkok's
published boundaries and Tokyo's labeled 800 m orientation circle. Compact map
cards share the same hierarchy across desktop and mobile, with their actions
kept accessible while longer content scrolls.

Language changes retain preferences, destination and comparison selections.
Browser Back restores the settings from that history entry. Missing monthly
rents are labeled unavailable; Tokyo and Bangkok rent definitions stay separate.
Tokyo map cards clear their selection when closed and reopen when the same
station is selected again.
Existing transport details, local notes, institutions, environmental context,
photos and source information remain available in expandable sections.

## Compatibility and data limits

Existing area URLs and ranking formulas remain compatible. The crowd-based
ranking is now labeled “Less crowding”; it is separate from the outdoor
quietness model.

This release changes the interface, not the source snapshots. Rent may be a
station average, municipal fallback, district estimate or asking-rent proxy.
The guides identify those differences and retain source dates and available
sample counts. Asking-rent medians are not lease prices or representative
fixed-size market rents. Commute times are estimates rather than live routing.

Scores remain relative within each city. Bangkok safety and district rents
retain their editorial limits; fine-grained maps do not imply equally precise
evidence. See the in-app methodology for source attribution and coverage.

No database migration or user action is required.
